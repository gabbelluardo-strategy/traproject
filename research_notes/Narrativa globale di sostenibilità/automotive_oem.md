## Q1. Core narrative/claim, link to business strategy, and consistency over time

### Takeaway
The narratives that last are the ones that put sustainability inside the **product or the industrial system**: BMW Neue Klasse/iFACTORY, Renault's circular-economy business unit, and Polestar's product carbon labels. Mercedes "Ambition 2039" is the longest-running claim, but its credibility suffered when the 2030 EV target was dropped. Volvo's "all-electric by 2030" was a strong claim and was withdrawn in 2024. Stellantis has also reset its own claim in 2026 (net zero 2038 → 2050), which makes the "coherence over time" theme sensitive for the client.

### Cited Findings

**Mercedes-Benz: "Ambition 2039"** (in use since 2019; the 2019 baseline is in the target definitions)
- Official framing is CO₂ reduction, not EV share. Versus 2019 the targets are: −90% in own production, −80% in use phase "where market conditions permit", and −70% across the value chain. Net zero by 2049 at the latest. — [Mercedes-Benz Group, Ambition 2039](https://group.mercedes-benz.com/sustainability/environment-climate/decarbonisation/ambition-2039.html); [Ambition 2039: our path to CO₂ neutrality](https://group.mercedes-benz.com/sustainability/environment-climate/decarbonisation/ambition-2039-our-path-to-co2-neutrality.html)
- In February 2024 Mercedes dropped the "all-electric by 2030 where market conditions allow" goal. It now says xEV (BEV+PHEV) could reach "up to 50%" of sales in the second half of the decade, and it will keep combustion models "well into the 2030s". — [electrive](https://www.electrive.com/2024/02/22/mercedes-drastically-electric-target-for-2030); [ESG Dive](https://www.esgdive.com/news/mercedes-benz-delays-electric-vehicle-goal/708803/); [newmobility.news](https://newmobility.news/2024/02/23/mercedes-gets-cold-feet-about-electrification-timing/)
- Electrified share of sales was 20.5% in 2025, up from 18.5% in 2024 (secondary summary). — [GreentechLead](https://greentechlead.com/sustainability/mercedes-benz-sustainability-strategy-2025-electrification-growth-e9-bn-investment-and-ambition-2039-net-zero-roadmap-53092)
- The new CLA is used as the product proof of the strategy. Mercedes says it wants to cut CO₂ per new car by up to 50% over the next decade. — [Automotive World (MB release)](https://www.automotiveworld.com/news-releases/mercedes-benz-reaffirms-its-commitment-to-sustainability-and-puts-ambitions-on-the-road-with-the-all-new-cla/)
- "Tomorrow XX" (revealed around December 2025) is a technology programme of more than 40 concepts to decarbonise components and materials. It extends the "XX" naming of Vision EQXX and Concept AMG GT XX. — [Automotive World](https://www.automotiveworld.com/news/tomorrow-xx-mercedes-benz-unveils-a-new-dimension-of-sustainability/); [auto motor und sport](https://www.auto-motor-und-sport.de/tech-zukunft/co2-fussabdruck-auto-produktion-co2-rucksack-e-auto-mercedes-tomorrow-xx/)
- Mercedes held a dedicated ESG conference for investors in 2022. — [MB IR ESG Conference 2022 deck (PDF)](https://group.mercedes-benz.com/dokumente/investoren/praesentationen/mercedes-benz-ir-esg-conference-2022-presentation.pdf)

**BMW Group: Neue Klasse + "Design for Circularity / Secondary First" + iFACTORY**
- iFACTORY was announced in 2022 as "LEAN. GREEN. DIGITAL.", a model for all plants and "not a one-off showpiece". The Neue Klasse is presented as the reason to "completely rethink automobile production". Debrecen is planned as the first car plant with no fossil energy in its production processes. — [BMW Group press, iFACTORY](https://www.press.bmwgroup.com/global/article/detail/T0384893EN/bmw-ifactory-lean-green-digital-master-plan-for-the-production-of-the-future)
- iX3 (first Neue Klasse model, start of production November 2025): "Design for Circularity" rests on three principles, one of which is "Secondary First". About one third of the iX3 50 xDrive's material is secondary. Supply-chain CO₂e fell 35% during development. Gen6 cells contain 50% secondary cobalt, lithium and nickel, with −42% CO₂e/Wh versus Gen5. The iX3 breaks even with an ICE model after about 21,500 km (EU mix) or 17,500 km (renewables). — [BMW Group press UK](https://www.press.bmwgroup.com/united-kingdom/article/detail/T0451729EN_GB/turning-vision-into-reality:-the-new-bmw-ix3-–-the-first-neue-klasse-model-drives-product-sustainability); [Automotive World copy](https://www.automotiveworld.com/news-releases/turning-vision-into-reality-the-new-bmw-ix3-the-first-neue-klasse-model-drives-product-sustainability/)
- Corporate "Transformation" page. — [bmwgroup.com/en/company/transformation.html](https://www.bmwgroup.com/en/company/transformation.html)

**Volkswagen Group: "regenerate+"** (presented in fiscal year 2024; "way to zero" was the earlier brand-level claim of the VW brand [unverified, not found in this search])
- regenerate+ is the group-wide strategy for all brands and subsidiaries, organised in four dimensions: Nature, People, Society, Business. Its stated ambition goes beyond "avoiding emissions" to having a "positive effect on people and nature". — [VW Group sustainability strategy](https://volkswagen-group.com/en/sustainability-strategy-18157); [Annual Report 2024, sustainability](https://annualreport2024.volkswagen-group.com/group-management-report/sustainable-value-enhancement/sustainability.html); [Annual Report 2025, strategy](https://annualreport2025.volkswagen-group.com/sustainability-report/general-information/business-model-value-chain-and-strategy.html)
- Targets: net carbon neutral by 2050; −30% CO₂ per customer-km by 2030 (vs 2018); all production sites net carbon neutral by 2040. 2024 results: −51% production GHG vs 2018 and 66.9% renewable electricity. — [GreentechLead](https://greentechlead.com/sustainability/volkswagen-sustainability-strategy-targets-net-zero-by-2050-with-90-emissions-reduction-100-renewable-electricity-and-40-circular-materials-usage-53735); [VW sustainability](https://www.volkswagen-group.com/en/sustainability-15772)
- The sources disagree on who is Chief Sustainability Officer: Dirk Voeste on the VW site, Dirk Große-Loheide per KnowESG. — [KnowESG](https://knowesg.com/social-governance/volkswagen-s-regenerate-strategy-where-sustainability-meets-purpose-and-profit)

**Renault Group: Renaulution (2021) → futuREady (10 March 2026)**
- futuREady succeeds Renaulution. Its aim is to become "the European automotive reference with global reach" and turn "a success story into a success system". Four pillars: growth ready, tech ready, excellence ready, trust ready. — [Renault Group magazine, futuREady](https://www.renaultgroup.com/en/magazine/our-group-news/futuready-turning-renault-groups-success-story-into-a-success-system/); [Renault Group page](https://www.renaultgroup.com/en/popin/revolution); [The Machine Maker](https://themachinemaker.com/news/renault-group-unveils-futuready-strategic-plan-to-shape-the-next-phase-of-growth/)
- Circular economy runs as a **business unit**. "The Future Is NEUTRAL" (launched 2022) covers the whole circular value chain: end-of-life vehicle collection and dismantling, parts reuse and remanufacturing, and recycling. Its target is more than €2.3bn turnover and over 10% operating margin by 2030 (sources disagree: ~€2bn per AMS). — [Circular Online](https://www.circularonline.co.uk/news/renault-group-launches-entity-to-create-automotive-circular-economy/); [Motor16](https://www.motor16.com/lo-mas-visto/renault-the-remakers-negocio-8200me/); [AMS](https://www.automotivemanufacturingsolutions.com/strategy/renault-advances-circular-economy-reuse-remake-recycle-business/532835)
- Remanufacturing at the Flins Refactory now sits inside The Future Is NEUTRAL as **THE REMAKERS** (9 product families, more than 11,000 items, parts about 30% cheaper than new; target +50% growth by 2030). At Automechanika in September 2026 the CEO said the circular economy is "no longer a vision but an industrial reality". — [Motorionline](https://www.motorionline.com/gruppo-renault-the-future-is-neutral-integra-il-business-di-remanufacturing/); [electrive Sept 2026](https://electrive.com/2026/09/08/renault-subsidiary-refurbishes-electric-motors); [newmobility Feb 2026](https://newmobility.news/en/2026/02/18/renaults-circular-shift-new-ceo-signals-industrial-ambition/)

**Volvo Cars: "fully electric by 2030" (withdrawn), climate action, net zero 2040**
- On 4 September 2024 Volvo abandoned 100% BEV by 2030. The new aim is 90–100% electrified (BEV+PHEV) with up to 10% mild hybrids. Shares fell about 7.5% that day. — [The Driven](https://thedriven.io/2024/09/05/volvo-cars-dumps-2030-all-electric-target-to-ramp-up-hybrids-as-it-weakens-climate-goals); [Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/money/2024/09/05/volvo-cars-abandons-2030-ev-only-target/149337); [Nasdaq](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/geely-owned-volvo-drops-electric-only-target-2030)
- Net zero across the value chain by 2040 is reaffirmed in the Annual & Sustainability Report 2025. The per-car 2030 target now appears as −65% to −75% vs 2018 (originally −75%), which suggests it was softened. — [Cision, Volvo AR&SR 2025](https://news.cision.com/volvo-car-ab--publ-/r/volvo-car-ab--publ--publishes-annual-and-sustainability-report-2025,c4317835); [Quartr ESG update](https://quartr.com/events/volvo-cars-volcar-esg-update_ozje01N3); [Volvo climate action page](https://volvocars.com/ie/v/sustainability/climate-action)

**Polestar: "Polestar 0 project"** (announced 2021)
- In the 2025 Sustainability Report (April 2026) Polestar moved its goal of a truly climate-neutral car from 2030 to **2035**. The Mission 0 House research consortium in Gothenburg was formally set up in 2025 with about SEK 100m over 5 years. Emissions per car sold are down 31% since 2020. The company aims to be climate-neutral by 2040. — [Automotive World](https://www.automotiveworld.com/news/polestar-revises-net-zero-car-deadline-to-2035/); [The Beep](https://thebeep.com.au/car-news/polestar-emissions-reduction-2025-sustainability-report)

**Stellantis (client baseline)**
- Under Dare Forward 2030 (2022) the targets were carbon net zero by 2038 and −50% by 2030. In early April 2026 the revised climate report moved net zero to **2050**, with −20/30% absolute Scope 1-3 by 2030 vs 2021. Milano Finanza links this to more than €22bn of write-downs, mostly EV-related. — [Milano Finanza](https://www.milanofinanza.it/news/stellantis-rinvia-di-12-anni-l-obiettivo-net-zero-la-svolta-del-ceo-antonio-filosa-con-il-nuovo-piano-202604071914094326); [Stellantis "Toward Carbon Net Zero"](https://www.stellantis.com/en/sustainability/toward-carbon-net-zero)
- Reported 2025 progress: −26% Scope 1-3 and −43% Scope 1-2 vs 2021. — [Stellantis, Toward Carbon Net Zero](https://www.stellantis.com/en/sustainability/toward-carbon-net-zero)
- **Outdated pages still live:** the old "carbon-net-zero-strategy" page and the Dare Forward "Care" pages (including Stellantis Brazil) still say 2038. — [stellantis.com carbon-net-zero-strategy](https://www.stellantis.com/en/sustainability/carbon-net-zero-strategy); [stellantis.com.br Dare Forward Care](https://www.stellantis.com.br/en/company/dare-forward-2030/care)
- New plan **FaSTLAne 2030** (Investor Day, 21 May 2026, Auburn Hills): €60bn, a multi-energy mix of 29 BEV, 15 PHEV/EREV, 24 hybrid and 39 ICE/MHEV models, the STLA One platform (800V), and BEVs under €15k from 2028 built in Pomigliano. — [Stellantis Investor Day 2026](https://www.stellantis.com/en/investors/events/stellantis-investor-day-2026); [electrive](https://www.electrive.com/2026/05/21/stellantis-plans-29-new-electric-vehicles-and-a-new-platform-for-all-drive-types/); [Stellantis media ES](https://www.media.stellantis.com/es-es/corporate/press/stellantis-presenta-un-plan-estrategico-de-60-000-millones-de-euros-para-acelerar-el-crecimiento-y-los-beneficios)
- SUSTAINera (2022) is the circular-economy label and business unit. Targets: more than €2bn revenue by 2030, 4× extended-life revenue, 10× recycling revenue vs 2021, reaffirmed at IARC 2024. SUSTAINera parts save "up to 80% material / 50% energy" based on LCA. — [Stellantis press 2022](https://www.stellantis.com/en/news/press-releases/2022/october/stellantis-fosters-circular-economy-ambitions-with-dedicated-business-unit-to-power-new-era-of-sustainable-manufacturing-and-consumption); [ESG Today](https://www.esgtoday.com/?p=10543); [autoinformed IARC 2024](https://autoinformed.com/stellantis-updates-circular-economy-initiatives-at-iarc/)

### Inferences

**Ranking of executions by distinctiveness**

| Rank | Execution | OEM | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Product sustainability declaration, a "nutrition label" for every car (cradle-to-gate t CO₂e + traced materials), plus a full LCA recalculated per model year | Polestar | **Top-tier** (category-defining) |
| 2 | Circular economy as a named, P&L-bearing business ("The Future Is NEUTRAL", Refactory Flins, THE REMAKERS) with its own revenue and margin targets | Renault | **Top-tier** (strategy-integrated) |
| 3 | Neue Klasse = product + plant + circularity in one story ("Secondary First", iFACTORY "Lean. Green. Digital.", TÜV-verified PCF report in the My BMW app) | BMW | **Top-tier** (strategy-integrated, credible) |
| 4 | Battery passport (QR code in the driver's door, built with Circulor), "world first" ahead of the 2027 EU mandate | Volvo | Strong, first-mover; becomes generic from February 2027 when it is mandatory |
| 5 | Model-level LCA reports (Volvo carbon footprint reports EX30/EX90; Mercedes "360° Environmental Check" TÜV-validated, run since around 2009 [start year unverified]) | Volvo / Mercedes | Good practice, now quasi-standard. Mercedes' long-running series is notable for consistency, not innovation |
| 6 | Polestar 0 "moonshot" (climate-neutral car) | Polestar | Distinctive as a narrative device, weakened by the slip from 2030 to 2035 |
| 7 | "Ambition 2039" long-running umbrella claim + "XX" family (EQXX → Tomorrow XX) | Mercedes | Consistent brand device, mid-tier: undermined by the EV retreat |
| 8 | regenerate+ (four-dimension framework) | VW | **Generic**: framework-style, no signature visual or product proof found |

- The common feature of the top tier is that **each claim can be checked against a physical product, plant or P&L line**: a label number, a business unit's revenue, a car's secondary-material share. Claims based on a date (Volvo 2030, Mercedes 2030, Stellantis 2038) turned out fragile in 2024-2026.
- For Stellantis, the lesson about coherence over time is to anchor the narrative on **proof points that survive a target reset**: SUSTAINera revenue, per-model footprint, plant-level renewables. A headline end-date is the part that does not survive a reset.

### Gaps
- Could not verify the exact launch year or usage span of VW's "way to zero" claim, or whether it has been retired.
- The Mercedes 2025 Sustainability Report primary text was not accessed. The figures come from secondary summaries and older official pages, and the percentages conflict (−70% value chain vs "up to −50% per car").
- The futuREady sustainability KPIs (CO₂ targets) were not found in the results.
- FaSTLAne 2030 sustainability/ESG content (if any) was not found. Search results cover only the product and financial targets. The Investor Day deck should be checked directly.

## Q2. Concrete formats and visual devices: which are innovative?

### Takeaway
The single most transferable device is the **product-level carbon label**: Polestar's "nutrition label", BMW's PCF in the app, Volvo's per-model carbon footprint report and battery passport QR. The second is a **named circular business** with its own brand: Renault's The Future Is NEUTRAL / Refactory / THE REMAKERS, which mirrors Stellantis SUSTAINera.

### Cited Findings
- **Polestar "We do believe in labels"**: each product carries a product sustainability declaration showing cradle-to-gate t CO₂e and traced materials, with recycled/renewable content and circularity to be added later. It is displayed in showrooms (Polestar Spaces) and on the website. Polestar 2 was 26 t CO₂e at launch. — [Polestar news "We do believe in labels"](https://www.polestar.com/global/news/we-do-believe-in-labels/); [about.polestar.com version](https://about.polestar.com/news/we-do-believe-in-labels)
- Polestar LCA hub: an LCA per model (Polestar 2, 3, 4, 5), recalculated every model year, with downloadable summaries and both cradle-to-gate and cradle-to-grave figures. — [Polestar LCA page](https://www.polestar.com/au/sustainability/reports/life-cycle-assessment); [Polestar reports hub](https://www.polestar.com/global/sustainability/reports/)
- The Polestar 3 cradle-to-gate footprint is 24.7 t CO₂e (vs 26.1 t for Polestar 2 at its 2020 launch). Material production and refining account for 68%. — [Polestar press (Prezly)](https://polestar.prezly.com/on-the-quest-for-performance-and-sustainable-mobility-polestar-3-cuts-its-carbon-footprint-to-247-tcoeby-reducing-aluminium-and-battery-related-emissions)
- Polestar co-wrote a "Pathway Report" with Rivian (an industry decarbonisation report). — [Polestar reports hub](https://www.polestar.com/us/sustainability/reports/)
- **Volvo EX30 carbon footprint report** (PDF, ISO 14067, 200,000 km): LFP version 18–31 t, NMC version 22–36 t depending on the electricity mix. The headline is "23 t, about 60% less than an XC40 ICE". The report shows the effect of charging with wind power versus the global or EU mix (−42% / −22%). — [Volvo EX30 carbon footprint report PDF](https://volvocars.com/assets/volvocm/globalpages/live/15A5B2C3FD154295815AED10F289AEAE/volvo_carbonfootprintreport_ex30.pdf); [Volvo media release](https://media.volvocars.com/global/en-gb/media/pressreleases/325272/volvo-ex30-lca-shows-lowest-carbon-footprint-of-any-fully-electric-volvo-car-to-date)
- **Volvo battery passport** (EX90, June 2024, with Circulor, blockchain-based): records raw-material origin, components, recycled content and carbon footprint. Owners see a simplified version through a QR code inside the driver's door, and regulators get the full version. Rollout to all EVs was promised. The EU makes battery passports mandatory from February 2027. — [newmobility.news](https://newmobility.news/2024/06/06/volvo-introduces-worlds-first-ev-battery-passport/); [Interesting Engineering](https://interestingengineering.com/transportation/volvo-battery-passport-ev-car); [CarsGuide](https://carsguide.com.au/car-news/game-changing-battery-tracing-technology-2025-volvo-ex90-launches-battery-passport-ahead-of)
- **BMW**: a TÜV-verified product carbon footprint report for the iX3 can be downloaded or viewed in the My BMW app. — [BMW press Ireland](https://www.press.bmwgroup.com/ireland/article/detail/T0451757EN/turning-vision-into-reality-the-new-bmw-ix3-%20-the-first-neue-klasse-model-drives-product)
- **Mercedes 360° Environmental Check**: a TÜV Süd-validated LCA per model (CLA with EQ Technology 2025, GLC, C-Class, and older B-Class and CLA). — [CLA with EQ Technology page](https://group.mercedes-benz.com/sustainability/environment-climate/decarbonisation/environmental-check/cla-with-eq-technology.html); [Lifecycle Compact CLA 2024 PDF](https://group.mercedes-benz.com/documents/sustainability/product/mercedes-benz-lifecycle-compact-cla-2024-en.pdf); [GLC check PDF](https://group.mercedes-benz.com/documents/sustainability/product/mercedes-benz-environmental-check-compact-glc.pdf)
- **Mercedes Tomorrow XX**: shown through tangible component stories, such as a screwed (not glued) headlight that almost doubles secondary content and nearly halves emissions, and brake pads with about 40% waste from old pads. — [Automotive World](https://www.automotiveworld.com/news/tomorrow-xx-mercedes-benz-unveils-a-new-dimension-of-sustainability/); [dealerfloor](https://dealerfloor.co.za/industry-news/mb-charts-a-circular-future-with-its-tomorrow-xx-sustainability-vision)
- **Renault**: the extra-financial ratings are shown as a table on the responsibility page. — [Renault Group responsibility page](https://www.renaultgroup.com/en/responsability)

### Inferences
- Polestar's label works because it **borrows a format consumers already know** (a food nutrition label) and puts one number at the point of sale. It is the clearest example of "simpler, more visual" that the client asked for.
- BMW's move of the PCF into the owner app, and Volvo's QR in the door, show the **next step: the label goes into the customer's everyday touchpoints**. This is directly relevant to Stellantis brand apps across its 14 brands.
- Because the EU battery passport becomes mandatory in February 2027, a Stellantis passport will not be distinctive on its own. Distinctiveness would come from **how it is presented to consumers** (design and brand storytelling), which is where Polestar remains the reference.
- I found no evidence of award-winning interactive annual-report microsites among these OEMs. The innovation in this sector is at **product level**, not in report formats.

### Gaps
- Image URLs for the Polestar label and the Volvo report covers could not be extracted (fetch and screenshots blocked). Suggested manual captures: the Polestar label page, the first pages of the Volvo EX30 PDF (headline chart), and the Mercedes CLA environmental check page.
- No current (2026) confirmation that the Polestar label is still displayed in the same format, or that the Volvo passport QR is live on the EX30.
- No award data found (Red Dot, Cannes Lions, Report Watch) for any of these executions.

## Q3. Adaptation to stakeholders and markets

### Takeaway
The best practice is **one claim, several layers**:
- consumers: label, QR or app
- investors: business-unit revenue and margin (Renault), a dedicated ESG conference (Mercedes 2022)
- regulators: full passport or LCA

Evidence of market-specific adaptation (EU vs US vs China) is thin in public sources.

### Cited Findings
- Volvo's battery passport has two layers by design: a simplified owner version (QR) and a full version for regulators. — [newmobility.news](https://newmobility.news/2024/06/06/volvo-introduces-worlds-first-ev-battery-passport/)
- Renault turns its circular narrative into investor terms (revenue above €2.3bn and margin above 10% by 2030), and into customer terms (remanufactured parts about 30% cheaper). — [Circular Online](https://www.circularonline.co.uk/news/renault-group-launches-entity-to-create-automotive-circular-economy/); [Motorionline](https://www.motorionline.com/gruppo-renault-the-future-is-neutral-integra-il-business-di-remanufacturing/)
- Mercedes ran a dedicated IR ESG Conference in 2022. — [MB IR ESG Conference 2022 PDF](https://group.mercedes-benz.com/dokumente/investoren/praesentationen/mercedes-benz-ir-esg-conference-2022-presentation.pdf)
- Mercedes conditions its use-phase targets on "market conditions" and describes a technology-neutral pathway (BEV/PHEV/ICE) for flexibility across global markets. — [Ambition 2039 page](https://group.mercedes-benz.com/sustainability/environment-climate/decarbonisation/ambition-2039.html); [GreentechLead](https://greentechlead.com/sustainability/mercedes-benz-sustainability-strategy-2025-electrification-growth-e9-bn-investment-and-ambition-2039-net-zero-roadmap-53092)
- VW's regenerate+ has a "People" dimension (culture, workforce, occupational safety, health), which gives an employee-facing entry point inside the corporate framework. — [VW Annual Report 2024](https://annualreport2024.volkswagen-group.com/group-management-report/sustainable-value-enhancement/sustainability.html)
- FaSTLAne 2030 is framed regionally: a return to core products in North America, with a distinctive BEV emphasis in Europe. — [Benchmark Minerals](https://source.benchmarkminerals.com/article/life-in-the-fastlane-stellantis-investor-day-2026); [The Clinic](https://www.theclinic.cl/2026/05/25/plan-estrategico-stellantis-2030/amp/)
- Polestar's label pages are localised across markets (US, UK, AU, CA, IE, NZ, etc.). — [Polestar US](https://www.polestar.com/us/news/we-do-believe-in-labels); [Polestar NZ Polestar 2 sustainability](https://polestar.com/nz/polestar-2/sustainability)

### Inferences
- "Multi-energy" is now the industry's real stance (Mercedes, Volvo and Stellantis FaSTLAne). A narrative built on **decarbonisation per product and circularity** travels better across Europe, North America, Brazil and the Middle East & Africa than one built on an EV-share promise. Example: in Brazil, flex-fuel/ethanol hybrids can be told as decarbonisation [background knowledge; Stellantis Bio-Hybrid is unverified here].
- Stellantis could use a Volvo-style **two-layer model**: one consumer-facing label or QR per model across brands, with the full LCA or passport for regulators and investors.

### Gaps
- No evidence found of China-specific sustainability communication by these OEMs.
- No employee-campaign evidence found (for example, internal sustainability ambassadors at BMW or Renault). The HR-communication angle needs a dedicated search.

## Q4. Effectiveness evidence and criticism / retreat

### Takeaway
Retreat is the dominant risk theme of 2024-2026:
- Mercedes (February 2024) and Volvo (September 2024) dropped their all-EV 2030 goals.
- Polestar moved Polestar 0 to 2035 (April 2026).
- Stellantis moved net zero 2038 → 2050 (April 2026).

Hard effectiveness metrics (engagement, awards) for the communication executions were not found. Rating evidence exists only for Renault.

### Cited Findings
- Mercedes' CEO cancelled the 2030 EV target. ## Q5. Transferable lessons for Stellantis (and how Stellantis communicates today)

### Takeaway
Stellantis today has a **strong circular asset (SUSTAINera)** but a **fragmented and partly outdated corporate narrative**: 2038 pages are still live next to 2050 pages, and the Dare Forward language sits alongside FaSTLAne 2030. The best benchmarks suggest building the narrative on product-level proof and a P&L circular business, not on end-dates.

### Cited Findings
- Stellantis' corporate sites contradict each other on the net-zero year (2038 vs 2050), including on the Brazilian site. — [stellantis.com carbon-net-zero-strategy](https://www.stellantis.com/en/sustainability/carbon-net-zero-strategy); [Toward Carbon Net Zero](https://www.stellantis.com/en/sustainability/toward-carbon-net-zero); [stellantis.com.br](https://www.stellantis.com.br/en/responsibility/carbon-net-zero-strategy)
- FaSTLAne 2030 is a multi-energy plan with 29 BEVs and BEVs under €15k from 2028. — [electrive](https://www.electrive.com/2026/05/21/stellantis-plans-29-new-electric-vehicles-and-a-new-platform-for-all-drive-types/)
- The SUSTAINera revenue target and LCA-based savings claims already exist. — [ESG Today](https://www.esgtoday.com/?p=10543); [Stellantis 2022 press release](https://www.stellantis.com/en/news/press-releases/2022/october/stellantis-fosters-circular-economy-ambitions-with-dedicated-business-unit-to-power-new-era-of-sustainable-manufacturing-and-consumption)
- Renault shows a comparable circular unit turned into a branded, investor-grade story (The Future Is NEUTRAL / Refactory / THE REMAKERS). — [Motorionline](https://www.motorionline.com/gruppo-renault-the-future-is-neutral-integra-il-business-di-remanufacturing/)

### Inferences (proposals)
1. **"Label for every Stellantis car" (from Polestar, BMW and Volvo).** A common, simple footprint label (cradle-to-gate t CO₂e, % recycled content, SUSTAINera-eligible parts) with one design system across the 14 brands. Show it in configurators, brand apps and dealers, starting with the STLA One / sub-€15k BEVs. This is the top-tier transferable device.
2. **Make SUSTAINera the "The Future Is NEUTRAL" of Stellantis (from Renault).** Report SUSTAINera as a business: revenue progress toward €2bn, margin, plants, jobs. Give it physical icons, as Renault does with the Flins Refactory. This connects ESG to FaSTLAne and to the investor story, and the jobs angle gives HR communication material.
3. **Proof over promise (lesson from the Mercedes, Volvo, Polestar and Stellantis resets).** After the 2038 → 2050 reset, lead with annual measurable proof points: −43% Scope 1-2 already achieved, renewable share, SUSTAINera revenue. The target date should not be the headline. Clean up outdated 2038 pages to avoid easy greenwashing accusations.
4. **Two-layer disclosure (from Volvo's battery passport).** Simple for customers and employees, full for regulators and investors. Prepare a branded consumer layer for the mandatory EU battery passport (February 2027) so a compliance obligation becomes a visible brand moment.
5. **Plant-level storytelling (from BMW iFACTORY).** Use flagship plants (for example, Pomigliano for the sub-€15k BEV) as tangible symbols of "lean, green, digital", which also suits employee communication.
