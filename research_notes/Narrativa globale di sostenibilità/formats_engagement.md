# Innovative formats for sustainability communication & employee engagement (one narrative, many audiences)

**Method note and limits (read first).** Research was done on 14 Oct 2026 using web search only. In this environment, WebFetch and direct HTTP (curl/Playwright) to company sites (stellantis.com, se.com, circulor.com, report.telekom.com, milanofinanza.it, antennagroup.com) were **blocked by the network proxy** (DNS failure / "403 CONNECT policy denial"). So **no screenshots were taken** and no `fmt_*.png` files exist. Findings below come from search-result extracts of the cited pages, not full-page reads. The URLs are the original source pages and the writer or client can open them directly. Most effectiveness metrics are self-reported by the companies.

**Critical context flag for the Stellantis brief:** the brief's "Carbon Net Zero 2038" target is **outdated**. In early April 2026 Stellantis moved net zero to **2050** and set a 2030 interim target of **-20% to -30% absolute GHG vs 2021**. Any narrative proposal must start from this (see Q5).

## Q1. What are the standout formats/devices, and why are they distinctive (not basic)?

### Takeaway
Six executions clear the "no basics" bar. Each turns ESG into a **repeatable device** rather than a document:
1. Schneider's single quarterly "Impact score", reported with financial results and tied to bonuses.
2. Danone's "One Person, One Voice, One Share", which made every employee a shareholder with a voice.
3. Volvo Cars/Polestar's per-car battery passport (QR code) and Product Sustainability Declaration.
4. Deutsche Telekom-style layered reporting: a CSRD statement plus a readable CR "companion" with an interactive KPI tool.
5. Climate Fresk run at industrial scale through in-house facilitators and ambassador networks (Société Générale, Renault Trucks, OPmobility).
6. As an emerging and weakly evidenced option: an AI "ESG assistant" on the report.

### Cited Findings

**Case 1: Schneider Electric. The quarterly "Impact score" as a single ESG KPI (SSI 2021-2025, now "Impact 2030")**
- Format: a composite score out of 10 built from a basket of indicators (21 in the original framework), **published every quarter alongside financial results** and **audited annually by a third party** — [Schneider newsletter 2019](https://www.se.com/ww/en/assets/564/document/54103/07-sustainability-newsletter-september-2019.pdf); [BusinessWire, SSI midterm 2023](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20230731755089/en/5495877/Schneider-Electric-Sustainability-Impact-program-reaches-midterm-milestone)
- Example of the format: "the overall SSI score came in at 5.28, on track to reach the 2023 end-year target of 6 out of 10" — [Schneider press release (se.com/eg)](https://www.se.com/eg/en/about-us/newsroom/news/press-releases/schneider-electric-sustainability-impact-program-reaches-midterm-milestone-6613d4c376f1a5f05307ecb7/)
- Link to pay: the FY2022 SSI score (4.91/10 vs a 4.70 target) fed the **short-term incentives of more than 64,000 managers** — [BusinessWire, FY2022 results](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20230216005832/en/Schneider-Electric-closes-2022-with-strong-Sustainability-Impact-results). In 2019 the STIP covered 52,000+ employees and the LTIP 2,000+ managers — [Schneider newsletter 2019](https://www.se.com/ww/en/assets/564/document/54103/07-sustainability-newsletter-september-2019.pdf). (A "20% weighting" figure often quoted could NOT be verified.)
- **Update for 2026, flagged as current:** SSI 2021-2025 has been replaced by **"Impact 2030"**. It has four pillars ("electrifying the world, reinventing the industry, unlocking human potential, empowering local communities") and the same quarterly score mechanic: Q1 2026 at 3.40/10 and H1 2026 at 3.69/10, against a 2026 target of 4.20 and a 2030 ambition of 10/10 — [DataCentreNews UK](https://datacentrenews.uk/story/schneider-electric-s-impact-2030-score-rises-to-3-40); [The Diplomat (RO), 11 May 2026](https://www.thediplomat.ro/2026/05/11/schneider-electric-reports-strong-early-progress-under-new-sustainability-roadmap-impact-2030-p/); [Automation Magazine, H1 2026](https://www.automationmagazine.co.uk/schneider-electric-reports-strong-first-half-year-results-under-new-sustainability-roadmap-impact-2030/)
- Related employee device: **Schneider Sustainability School**. It was first launched internally "to educate Schneider Electric employees to better support the company's partner ecosystem". In May 2023 it opened free to outside companies — [Schneider press release](https://www.se.com/ww/en/about-us/newsroom/news/press-releases/schneider-electric%E2%80%99s-first-sustainability-school-opens-for-enrolment-646b77fd7854e9e9160ca954); [Schneider Perspectives video page](https://perspectives.se.com/youtube-energy-and-sustainability-services/schneider-electric-sustainability-school-accelerating-the-journey-to-net-zero-schneider-electric)
- Why distinctive: one number, a fixed quarterly rhythm, the same stage as financial results, and money attached. ESG becomes a management KPI that employees, investors and media all read the same way.

**Case 2: Danone, "One Person, One Voice, One Share" (launched 2018)**
- Format: every employee received **one Danone share** plus an amplified dividend-based incentive. This was paired with the "One Voice" global employee survey/consultation feeding into the 2030 goals — [Danone Nordics careers page](https://www.careers.danone.com/nordics-baltics/en/about-us/our-promise/people-centric.html); [ICMR case study](https://icmrindia.org/Casestudies/catalogue/Human%20Resource%20and%20Organization%20Behavior/HROB259.htm)
- Scale: more than 86,000 employees took part in the last "One Voice" edition — [WTW case](https://wtwco.com/en-ae/insights/2021/09/how-employee-insights-power-danones-sustainable-growth-agenda). The careers page states 87,000 employees are shareholders (undated) — [Danone careers](https://www.careers.danone.com/nordics-baltics/en/about-us/our-promise/people-centric.html)
- Claimed outcomes: employee input helped bring the B Corp certification deadline forward to 2025 from 2030 — [WTW case](https://wtwco.com/en-ae/insights/2021/09/how-employee-insights-power-danones-sustainable-growth-agenda). This is a consultancy write-up and is not independently verified.
- Governance link: at the 2019 AGM, shareholders approved all resolutions and Danone said a global employee share subscription plan would follow — [GlobeNewswire 2019](https://www.globenewswire.com/de/news-release/2019/04/25/1809899/0/en/Danone-2019-Danone-Shareholders-Meeting-approval-of-all-resolutions.html)
- **Dating flag:** the programme dates from 2018-2021 and sits in the Faber era (Faber was ousted in 2021). Current status and strength are not verified for 2026. Present it as a design precedent, not a live benchmark.

**Case 3: Volvo Cars EX90 battery passport plus Polestar Product Sustainability Declaration (automotive product-level transparency)**
- Volvo EX90 (announced June 2024, with Circulor): billed as the "world's first EV battery passport". Owners reach a **simplified passport by scanning a QR code on the driver's door frame or through the Volvo Cars app**. It shows the origin of cobalt, nickel, graphite and lithium, the embedded carbon footprint of the battery pack, the recycled content and state of health. A fuller version goes to regulators. Circulor's CEO put the cost at about $10 per car, with health data for 15 years — [Circulor](https://circulor.com/worlds-first-battery-passport); [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/vehicle-tech/hybrid-electric-vehicles/volvos-new-ev-has-the-worlds-first-battery-passport-heres-why-thats-a-big-deal); [BusinessDay/Reuters](https://www.businessday.co.za/bd/companies/industrials/2024-06-05-volvo-launches-ev-battery-passport-for-ex90-suv/)
- Regulatory hook: battery passports become mandatory for EV batteries sold in the EU from **February 2027** — [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/vehicle-tech/hybrid-electric-vehicles/volvos-new-ev-has-the-worlds-first-battery-passport-heres-why-thats-a-big-deal)
- Polestar's PSD gives a per-model cradle-to-gate carbon footprint (Polestar 2 MY2023: 25.4 / 24.4 / 23.5 tCO2e by variant) plus traceability (Circulor blockchain for cobalt, extended to mica, nickel, lithium and others). It is shown as labelling on the website and in Polestar Spaces retail — [Polestar 2 PSD pdf](https://www.polestar.com/dato-assets/11286/1668601346-polestar2_psd_w46_2022.pdf); [Polestar NZ sustainability page](https://polestar.com/nz/polestar-2/sustainability); [WardsAuto](https://wardsauto.com/electric/blockchain-to-help-polestar-track-battery-material-sources). Caveat: the blockchain announcements date from 2021, and current progress was not verified.
- Non-auto comparator: Logitech pledged in June 2020 to put lifecycle carbon labels (kg CO2e) on packaging and web pages across its whole portfolio by 2025, with DEKRA verification. Whether it fully achieved this is unconfirmed — [BusinessWire 2020](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20200617005146/en/Logitech-Pledges-Carbon-Impact-Transparency); [CSRwire](https://csrwire.com/press-release/logitech-and-product-carbon-impact-labeling/)
- Why distinctive: ESG moves from the corporate report to the **object the customer buys**, at the point of sale and in the car itself.

**Case 4: Layered "CSRD statement + readable companion + interactive KPI tool" (Deutsche Telekom; Anora as a mid-cap model)**
- Deutsche Telekom: the CR report is explicitly designed as a **companion to the CSRD sustainability statement**. The statement holds the detailed materiality process, while the CR report covers other ESG topics stakeholders care about. Its homepage offers a highlights overview and an **interactive KPI tool** — [Telekom CR Report 2025, "About this report"](https://report.telekom.com/cr-report/2025/cr-strategy/about-this-report.html)
- Anora (Finland): the CSRD statement sits in the Board of Directors' report, plus a separate "Sustainability Review" for an "easy-to-read overview". 2025 was the second year of the model — [Anora](https://anoraindustrial.com/en/anoras-sustainability-reports-2025-are-out)
- Market context: EFRAG/PwC/BITC reviews of first-wave CSRD statements find them long and uneven. Good practice includes tight cross-referencing and concise IRO tables (e.g. Glenveagh) — [BITC Ireland](https://bitc.ie/learnings-from-the-first-csrd-compliant-sustainability-statements/); [PwC Greece](https://www.pwc.com/gr/en/First_Greek_CSRD_Reporters_FY24.pdf)
- Why distinctive: once CSRD turns the statement into a compliance document, the companion becomes the place where the narrative lives. This is the cleanest "one narrative, many audiences" architecture.

**Case 5: Climate Fresk at industrial scale through in-house facilitators and ambassador networks**
- Société Générale: **more than 40,000 employees** took part in about 18 months (around 30% of staff, target reached ahead of schedule), delivered by **750 in-house facilitators**. It is part of the core training plan — [Société Générale, 4 Jul 2024](https://www.societegenerale.com/en/news/all-news/objective-surpassed-over-40000-employees-trained-climate-fresk)
- Renault Trucks (a Volvo Group company, despite the Renault name): 2,800 employees had taken part, with more than 50 internal facilitators — [Renault Trucks "Good Move" pdf](https://renault-trucks.com/sites/corporate/files/2024-07/RenaultTrucks_GoodMove018_%20Fresques%20Climat_EN.pdf)
- OPmobility (ex-Plastic Omnium, an automotive supplier and close peer of Stellantis): a **Sustainability Ambassadors network of 300+ volunteer employees** who "set up, participate, promote and share sustainable development initiatives … such as the organization of The Climate Fresk". It sits within the group's "ACT FOR ALL" programme (people / responsibly / planet) — [OPmobility ambassadors page](https://www.opmobility.com/en/sustainability-ambassadors-en/); [OPmobility sustainability](https://www.opmobility.com/en/sustainability/)
- Other benchmarks: Bouygues reports about 10,000 employees trained (search extract; primary page not retrieved). Berluti reports 100% of France staff and 59% abroad by end-2023 — [Berluti](https://www.berluti.com/en-cn/sustainable-development/communities/). The format is a 3-hour workshop for groups of 4-7 participants.
- Critical view: Reporterre described the Fresque as "the game the CAC 40 likes" — [Reporterre](https://reporterre.net/Fresque-du-climat-le-jeu-qui-plait-au-Cac40). The risk is awareness without action unless it is linked to job-specific follow-ups.
- No reliable figures were found for Renault Group, Capgemini or L'Oréal all-staff Fresk claims, so do not cite them.

**Case 6 (emerging, weak evidence): an AI ESG assistant on the report**
- Asana launched an "AI ESG Helper" chatbot that lets non-experts query its ESG report and strategy — [ESG MENA](https://esgmena.com/asana-launches-esg-chatbot/); [OneStopESG](https://onestopesg.com/esg-news/asana-unveils-ai-esg-helper-a-new-era-for-corporate-sustainability-1725284302779). There is no primary press release and launch timing is unconfirmed. No large-cap industrial example was found.

### Inferences
- The strongest cases share one trait: **a device that repeats** (a quarterly score, a share per employee, a QR code per car, a 3-hour workshop per employee), not a one-off campaign.
- For an automaker, Cases 3 and 4 are the most "business-native", because they speak through products and through the reporting cycle investors already follow.

### Gaps
- Screenshots and direct visual URLs could not be captured because the proxy blocked the sites. The writer should open the URLs above.
- No verified, current (2025-26) data was found on Unilever, SAP, Salesforce or Accenture gamified employee apps. They were left out on purpose rather than cited weakly.

## Q2. How do these formats connect ESG to business strategy and stay coherent over time?

### Takeaway
Coherence comes from **naming plus cadence plus money**. Schneider gives its sustainability roadmap a brand name, reports one score with the financials every quarter and pays bonuses on it. Danone tied employee ownership to its 2030 goals. Volvo/Polestar make the product itself the proof.

### Cited Findings
- Schneider's "Impact 2030" pillars are worded as business lines ("electrifying the world, reinventing the industry"), not as ESG categories, and the score continues the 2021-2025 mechanic — [Automation Magazine](https://www.automationmagazine.co.uk/schneider-electric-reports-strong-first-half-year-results-under-new-sustainability-roadmap-impact-2030/); [DataCentreNews](https://datacentrenews.uk/story/schneider-electric-s-impact-2030-score-rises-to-3-40)
- Schneider ties the score to the incentives of 64,000+ managers — [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20230216005832/en/Schneider-Electric-closes-2022-with-strong-Sustainability-Impact-results)
- All CAC 40 companies now include at least one ESG metric in executive bonus or LTI. GHG reduction is the most common metric — [Glass Lewis](https://www.glasslewis.com/article/esg-criteria-in-the-compensation-of-cac-40-executives). A French study found 17 companies with ESG criteria in **all-employee** profit-sharing schemes, mostly CO2 indicators — [Linfodurable/Ecofi](https://www.linfodurable.fr/partenaires/ecofi/les-criteres-esg-dans-les-remunerations-ne-font-plus-debat-43857)
- Danone launched "One Person, One Voice, One Share" together with its 2030 goals — [DairyReporter 2018](https://www.dairyreporter.com/Article/2018/04/30/Danone-sets-2030-goals/)
- Volvo's battery passport anticipates the EU Battery Regulation (Feb 2027). This turns compliance into a customer-facing advantage — [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/vehicle-tech/hybrid-electric-vehicles/volvos-new-ev-has-the-worlds-first-battery-passport-heres-why-thats-a-big-deal)

### Inferences
- Schneider's renewal from SSI to Impact 2030 shows how to keep a recognisable device across a strategy reset: the score and its rhythm stay, while the pillars change. This is directly relevant to Stellantis, which is moving from "Dare Forward 2030" to a new plan.

### Gaps
- No verified data on whether Schneider's score weighting in the STIP changed under Impact 2030.

## Q3. How do they adapt to stakeholders and markets? (including greenhushing vs CSRD)

### Takeaway
The layered model serves each audience through a separate device:
- Investors: the CSRD statement and the quarterly score.
- Customers: product passports and labels.
- Employees: workshops, shares and ambassadors.
- Local communities: local volunteering and local facilitators.

In 2025-26 the US/EU split is handled through a mix of reduced visibility and reframing. Evidence shows most large firms kept or sped up their climate work while talking about it less.

### Cited Findings
- Greenhushing at scale: 63 of the UK's 100 largest listed firms under-promoted their environmental work in 2024, and as many as 67 major US firms did the same (Connected Impact) — [CleanTechnica, 28 Oct 2025](https://cleantechnica.com/2025/10/28/greenhushing-when-companies-dont-want-to-publicize-their-climate-progress)
- A Harvard study of 75 global firms found only 13% scaled back their sustainability efforts or messaging, while 32% accelerated them — as summarised by [Antenna Group, "Sustainability could use a rebrand in 2026"](https://www.antennagroup.com/signals/sustainability-could-use-a-rebrand-in-2026) (secondary; original HBR piece not retrieved)
- Legal double bind: companies "can be sued for saying too little – and sued for saying too much" (South Pole commentary). The EU Empowering Consumers Directive (2024/825) tightens green claims — [ClimatePartner glossary](https://www.climatepartner.com/index%2Ephp/en/knowledge/glossary/greenhushing)
- Climate Impact X CEO: greenhushing "doesn't help the financial system mobilize capital" — [Bloomberg Gov](https://news.bgov.com/environment-and-energy/greenhushing-can-keep-costs-higher-climate-impact-x-ceo-says)
- Deutsche Telekom's companion CR report and KPI tool sit beside the CSRD statement (separate depth levels for different readers) — [Telekom](https://report.telekom.com/cr-report/2025/cr-strategy/about-this-report.html)
- Webranking 2025-26 (Comprend) analysed 638 European corporate sites. Iberdrola was the only Spanish company in the European top 10, with its continuous improvement in sustainability content cited — [Iberdrola newsroom](https://www.iberdrola.com/sala-comunicacao/noticia/detalhe/iberdrola-unica-empresa-espanhola-dez-melhor-site-corporativo-toda-europa). In Webranking (Lundquist/Comprend), Poste Italiane scored highest in Europe for strategy and sustainability content — [TG Poste, Dec 2024](https://tgposte.poste.it/en/2024/12/10/communication-poste-italiane-among-europes-leaders/)

### Inferences
- The practical answer to the US/EU divide is a **single fact base with market-specific framing**. The same data (energy and cost efficiency, supply-chain resilience, battery traceability) is told as "efficiency/affordability/energy security" in North America and as "transition/CSRD" in the EU. This is an inference: there are no direct sourced corporate examples of "energy security" reframing (see Gaps).
- Product passports work around the divide. A traceable battery is a quality, safety and resilience story in the US and a compliance and climate story in the EU.

### Gaps
- No primary corporate example was found of explicit "climate → energy security/affordability" reframing in 2025-26 (the search returned only commentary).
- Lundquist .future/.trust 2025 rankings for sustainability communication could not be retrieved. The only 2025 .trust item found is a finance edition on AI and cyber, per [Lundquist news](https://lundquist.it/our-news/page/2/). Report Watch 2025 rankings were not found.
- No verified country-level localisation case (Brazil/India/US microsites) was found within the budget.

## Q4. Evidence of effectiveness

### Takeaway
The hardest evidence is participation and scale data, which is self-reported: Société Générale's 40,000 employees and 750 facilitators, Danone's 86,000 respondents and about 87,000 employee shareholders, and Schneider's audited score with 64,000+ managers on incentives. For product devices, evidence is limited to launch and regulatory fit.

### Cited Findings
- Société Générale: 40,000+ participants and 750 facilitators in 18 months — [SG](https://www.societegenerale.com/en/news/all-news/objective-surpassed-over-40000-employees-trained-climate-fresk)
- Danone: 86,000+ One Voice participants — [WTW](https://wtwco.com/en-ae/insights/2021/09/how-employee-insights-power-danones-sustainable-growth-agenda)
- Schneider: score above target (4.91 vs 4.70 in FY2022), third-party audited — [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20230216005832/en/Schneider-Electric-closes-2022-with-strong-Sustainability-Impact-results)
- OPmobility ambassador network grew from 200+ to 300+ members — [OPmobility](https://www.opmobility.com/en/sustainability-ambassadors-en/)
- Volvo passport: no customer usage metrics found. Treat it as an announced capability — [Circulor](https://circulor.com/worlds-first-battery-passport)

### Inferences
- For Stellantis, KPIs should be defined from the start (participation %, facilitators per site, passport scans, companion report reads) so the programme can enter awards such as Ragan CSR, PRNEWS Impact and Corporate Content Awards.

### Gaps
- No award wins (Cannes Lions, IABC Gold Quill, ARC) were verified for the cases above. Ragan's 2025 CSR Awards included Hyundai Motor America's "Hyundai Hope" under CSR/ESG Initiatives — [Ragan](https://www.ragan.com/celebrating-the-winners-of-ragans-2025-csr-awards-see-the-list/). This is the only automotive award signal found, and it is a philanthropy programme, not a format.

## Q5. Stellantis today, and what could concretely be proposed

### Takeaway
Stellantis already has two strong but **narratively disconnected** employee assets: Motor Citizens volunteering and the Shares to Win share plan. It has also had a **climate-target reset in 2026** (net zero moved from 2038 to 2050). That makes a recognisable, coherent device more urgent, not less.

### Cited Findings (Stellantis current state)
- First CSRD/ESRS report: the "Expanded Sustainability Statement 2024", published February 2025, includes an "Own Workforce" section. Almost 6,000 colleagues volunteered through **Motor Citizens**, and the **Shares to Win** plan was extended to nearly the entire global workforce. Women hold 33.5% of leadership positions — [Stellantis ESS 2024 pdf](https://www.stellantisandyou.com/content/dam/stellantis-and-you/website/privacy_e_legal_notes/Stellantis_Expanded_Sustainability_Statement_2024.pdf); [sustainabilityreports.com](https://www.sustainabilityreports.com/stellantis/2024/expanded-sustainability-statement)
- 2023 CSR report (April 2024): CSR was embedded in Dare Forward 2030, with a -12.6% absolute carbon footprint vs 2021. 5,174 employees volunteered in 2023 — [Stellantis press release](https://www.stellantis.com/en/news/press-releases/2024/april/stellantis-2023-corporate-social-responsibility-report-outlines-strong-results-against-ambitious-dare-forward-2030-targets)
- Motor Citizens 2025: hours and volunteers both rose by more than 25% — [Stellantis media (FR)](https://www.media.stellantis.com/fr-fr/corporate/press/journee-internationale-de-l-education-le-programme-stellantis-philanthropy-renouvelle-son-engagement-en-faveur-des-partenariats-communautaires)
- Shares to Win 2025 (closed 25 Nov 2025): 20 countries and more than 235,000 eligible employees. Participation was about 11% (secondary source). Employees invested €141m and Stellantis matched €68m. Over three editions, more than 22m shares were subscribed. The plan offered a 20% discount and a match of up to €1,000 — [MarketScreener](https://in.marketscreener.com/news/stellantisa-shares-to-win-employee-purchase-plan-reaches-22-million-shares-subscribed-in-over-thre-ce7d51dcdd81f520); [MarketChameleon](https://marketchameleon.com/Blog/post/2025/12/05/stellantis-employees-ownership-plan-22-million-shares); [ClubAlfa](https://www.clubalfa.it/en/stellantis-launches-third-edition-of-employee-shareholding-program-shares-to-win-2025-24968)
- **Climate reset:** in early April 2026 Stellantis moved its net-zero target **from 2038 to 2050**, with **-20 to -30% absolute GHG by 2030 vs 2021**. This followed write-downs of more than €22bn linked largely to EVs, ahead of the Filosa plan at the May 2026 Investor Day — [Milano Finanza](https://www.milanofinanza.it/news/stellantis-rinvia-di-12-anni-l-obiettivo-net-zero-la-svolta-del-ceo-antonio-filosa-con-il-nuovo-piano-202604071914094326); [Stellantis "Toward Carbon Net Zero" page](https://www.stellantis.com/en/sustainability/toward-carbon-net-zero); [ClubAlfa](https://www.clubalfa.it/en/stellantis-delays-its-green-targets-and-moves-net-zero-to-2050-35759). Critics read the new path as closer to 2°C than 1.5°C (ClubAlfa, opinion).

### Inferences: concrete proposals for Stellantis (for the consultancy to adapt)
1. **A "Stellantis Impact Score" (Schneider model).** A single audited index out of 10, built from 6-8 KPIs:
   - absolute CO2 vs 2021
   - SUSTAINera circular revenue / parts reman
   - LEV mix
   - safety
   - women in leadership
   - Motor Citizens hours

   Report it every quarter with the financial results, and link a slice of the management/all-employee bonus to it. Benefit: it gives the post-reset narrative a stable, recognisable device that survives the switch from Dare Forward 2030 to the new plan.
2. **Merge "Shares to Win" and Motor Citizens into one employee-ownership narrative (Danone model).** Example framing: "owners of the transition". Add a light "One Voice" consultation on how employees would deliver the new targets. Raising the ~11% participation rate is itself a measurable goal.
3. **Battery and vehicle passport as a brand-level customer device (Volvo/Polestar model).** The EU makes battery passports mandatory from Feb 2027. Stellantis can turn the obligation into a QR-based "product story" that each of the 14 brands tells in its own voice (Fiat/Citroën: affordability; Jeep/Ram in North America: durability and resilience; Peugeot/DS/Alfa: tech and quality). SUSTAINera reman and recycled content would appear on the same page.
4. **Layered reporting set (Telekom model).** Three layers:
   - the CSRD Sustainability Statement for compliance and investors
   - a 10-15 page "Essentials/Companion" with an interactive KPI tool for media, customers and employees
   - regional cuts (North America framed on efficiency, jobs and energy security; South America on local production, flex-fuel/biofuels and communities; Europe on CSRD and the transition)
5. **A Climate/Mobility Fresk ambassador network (Société Générale / OPmobility model).** Train in-house facilitators per plant and region and set a target share of employees reached. Add a job-specific follow-up module (design, purchasing, manufacturing, sales) so the workshop leads to action rather than awareness only. Note: the absence of Fresk data for Renault Group means no auto-OEM peer benchmark is verified.

### Gaps
- No evidence was found of a Stellantis employee climate-training programme, ESG weighting in all-employee variable pay, an interactive ESG data hub, or a 2025 CSR report published in 2026. A 2025 Annual Report exists — [sustainabilityreports.com](https://www.sustainabilityreports.com/stellantis/2025/annual-report-1) — but its contents were not verified.
- The content of the new Filosa strategic plan (May 2026 Investor Day), and whether "Dare Forward 2030" was formally retired, was not verified.
- The SUSTAINera status in 2026 was not verified.
