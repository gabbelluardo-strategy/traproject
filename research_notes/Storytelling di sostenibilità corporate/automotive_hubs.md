## Q1. Core storyline and organising framework per company

### Takeaway
Two families of narrative architecture emerge:
- **Framework-led hubs.** VW "regenerate+" (4 dimensions), Mercedes (6 strategic areas, with "Ambition 2039" as the climate claim), Polestar (4 pillars), Volvo (3 pillars), Toyota (6 challenges), Kia ("Plan S" plus ESG vision).
- **Single-idea / slogan-led hubs.** BYD "Cool the Earth by 1°C", Hyundai "Progress for Humanity", Renault "The Future is NEUTRAL" (circular business).

The VW reference page is a clean example of the first family. It has a memorable *name* (regenerate+) and a *4-dimension grid* tied to KPIs, but the visual or emotional signature behind it is weak or unverified.

  1. BYD "Cool the Earth by 1°C": one number, one image.

## Q2. Hub structure, depth layers and visual richness

### Takeaway
Most hubs follow a three-layer pattern:
1. Overview or claim page.
2. Pillar or dimension sub-pages.
3. Data and report layer (ESRS-integrated annual report, investor ESG page, PDFs).

The differences lie in whether a **product-proof layer** exists (Mercedes environmental checks, Polestar labels and LCAs, BMW product carbon footprint), and in whether the pillars are **mirrored across all assets**, as with VW.

Visual assessment could not be verified this round.

### Cited Findings
- **VW:** hub → strategy sub-page ("How do we do it?") → council sub-page. The deep layer is the Annual Report microsite with sections such as "business model, value chain and strategy", "introduction to environmental management" and "resource use and circular economy", plus a separate regenerate+ Progress Report. — [VW strategy](https://www.volkswagen-group.com/en/sustainability-strategy-18157); [AR2025 business model](https://annualreport2025.volkswagen-group.com/sustainability-report/general-information/business-model-value-chain-and-strategy.html); [AR2025 environmental mgmt](https://annualreport2025.volkswagen-group.com/sustainability-report/environmental-information/introduction-to-environmental-management.html); [AR2024 circular economy](https://annualreport2024.volkswagen-group.com/sustainability-report/environment/resource-use-and-circular-economy.html)
- **Mercedes:** hub → six strategic areas → Ambition 2039 → investor ESG and metrics summary PDF. There is also an interview format: "Sustainability – not just a trend". — [MB interview](https://group.mercedes-benz.com/responsibility/sustainability/interview-sustainability-not-just-a-trend.html); [MB ESG](https://group.mercedes-benz.com/investors/esg/)

### Inferences
- **Branded report titles** (Kia "MOVE", Polestar "The Beep Report", Hyundai "Road to Sustainability", VW "regenerate+ Progress Report") are a cheap way to make a compliance document feel like a story. Stellantis' CSR report has no such editorial brand [to verify].
