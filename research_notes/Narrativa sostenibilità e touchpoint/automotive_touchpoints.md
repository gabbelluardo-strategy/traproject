# Automotive brands: how sustainability shows up at concrete customer touchpoints (digital + physical), 2022-2026

Scope note: research done 14 Oct 2026. WebFetch was blocked (DNS failure on hyundai.news, dezeen.com), so all findings come from WebSearch result snippets plus their URLs; primary pages were not read in full. Corporate strategies (Polestar label, Volvo battery passport, BMW Neue Klasse/iFACTORY/My BMW app, Renault Refactory, Mercedes Ambition 2039, VW regenerate+) were deliberately not re-analysed. Coverage is uneven: Hyundai/Kia, NIO and Cupra are well sourced; several brands on the list (Rivian Spaces, Genesis, Toyota/Lexus, Porsche/Mercedes merchandise, Xpeng, Zeekr, Lucid, Tesla, Skoda, configurators and apps) could NOT be checked within the tool budget. See the Gaps sections.

## Q1. What exactly is the touchpoint execution, and how does it make sustainability tangible, simple and visual?

### Takeaway
The standout executions turn an abstract environmental programme into a single tangible object or a single "voice". Examples: Hyundai's trees that "speak" through live sensors and an LLM (Tree Correspondents), Hyundai naming unnamed underwater forests (Forests Without Names), Kia's EV3 trunk liner made from Great Pacific Garbage Patch plastic with a QR code built in, and NIO's Blue Sky Lab fashion made from seatbelts and airbags and sold inside NIO Houses. Hyundai Motor Group is the clearest benchmark for award-winning sustainability communication in 2025-2026.

### Cited Findings
**A. Hyundai "Tree Correspondents" (digital, AI, data; launched July 2025, 10th anniversary of IONIQ Forest)**
- Hyundai calls it an AI-driven campaign that tells the trees' story in the first person, using a bespoke large language model. — [Hyundai Brand Journal](https://www.hyundai.com/worldwide/en/brand-journal/sustainable-vision/tree-correspondents-campaign-cannes); [CleanTechnica](https://cleantechnica.com/2025/07/23/hyundai-motor-celebrates-10-years-of-ioniq-forest-with-tree-correspondents-campaign/)
- Trackers on trees in Brazil, the Czech Republic and Korea gather real-time ecological readings. The agency worked with Meter Group on live sensors in the IONIQ Forests. — [Muse by Clio (FCB New York case)](https://musebyclios.com/musings/how-fcb-n-y-used-ai-and-data-to-give-trees-a-voice/)
- Agency: FCB New York. The LLM was built with the partner Uncharted Limbo and combines live tree data, scientific insight and historical climate data. — [Muse by Clio](https://musebyclios.com/musings/how-fcb-n-y-used-ai-and-data-to-give-trees-a-voice/)
- IONIQ Forest has passed 1 million trees planted in 13 countries. — [CleanTechnica](https://cleantechnica.com/2025/07/23/hyundai-motor-celebrates-10-years-of-ioniq-forest-with-tree-correspondents-campaign/); [Hyundai Motor Group news](https://www.hyundaimotorgroup.com/en/news/CONT0000000000181663)

**B. Hyundai "Forests Without Names" (data, mapping, naming; 2026)**
- Hyundai gave names to overlooked underwater seaweed forests so these ecosystems become visible. It built a global marine-forest map from satellite seaweed data and protected-area information, covering unprotected sea forests in Korea, Argentina and Australia, and shared the data with a global marine-conservation network. — [Hyundai newsroom 2026](https://www.hyundai.com/worldwide/en/newsroom/detail/hyundai-motor-wins-two-cannes-lions-awards-including-grand-prix-for-second-consecutive-year-0000001210); [Hyundai EU news](https://www.hyundai.news/eu/articles/press-releases/cannes-lions-awards-2026.html); [Marketing Edge, secondary](https://marketingedge.com.ng/?p=201442)

**C. Hyundai "Coquí Alarmed" (the product itself as the medium; Puerto Rico, 2026)**
- Hyundai Puerto Rico replaced the lock/unlock sound of its local cars with the call of the coquí frog. Rental firms (AVIS, Enterprise, Charlie Car Rental) put these cars in tourists' hands. It is a biodiversity and cultural campaign, not an EV campaign. — [Hyundai newsroom 2026](https://www.hyundai.com/worldwide/en/newsroom/detail/0000001210); [LBB Online GP list](https://lbbonline.com/news/Cannes-Lions-2026-Grand-Prix-Winners-in-Audio-and-Radio-Creative-B2B-Creative-Brand-Health-and-Wellness-Outdoor-Pharma-Print-and-Publishing-and-the-Grand-Prix-for-Good)

**D. Kia x The Ocean Cleanup: EV3 trunk liner (product, accessory, dealer touchpoint)**
- Launched October 2024 as "the world's first car accessory made using plastic extracted from the Great Pacific Garbage Patch". It contains 40% recycled ocean plastic, and each liner has an embedded QR code that links to the product and partnership story. — [Kia EU press release](https://prod2-press.kia.com/eu/en/home/media-resouces/press-releases/2024/kia-ocean-clean-up.html); [AAP/Cision](https://www.aap.com.au/aapreleases/cision20241014ae29955)
- The partnership has run since 2022. Brand pages: [Kia EU – The Ocean Cleanup](https://www.kia.com/eu/about-kia/sustainability/the-ocean-cleanup/); [Kia Worldwide](https://worldwide.kia.com/int/sustainability/the-ocean-cleanup)
- Sold as an accessory at retail. A UK dealer lists it at £104 incl. VAT, with an offer ending 30 Sep 2026. — [Lloyd Motor Group](https://www.lloydmotorgroup.com/Kia/Current-Offers/The-Ocean-Clean-Up-Trunk-Liner/628)
- July 2026: Kia is supplying 2 EV3 and 2 EV4 to The Ocean Cleanup's field operations. The EV3s are fitted with the ocean-plastic liners. — [Kia Worldwide page](https://worldwide.kia.com/int/sustainability/the-ocean-cleanup) (from the search summary; verify the date on the page)
- NOTE: the brief mentioned "Hyundai IONIQ / The Ocean Cleanup". The searches found that The Ocean Cleanup partner is **Kia**. Hyundai's ocean partner is **Healthy Seas**: since 2021 it has run beach clean-ups and ghost-net recovery, with the nets regenerated into ECONYL for the IONIQ 5 floor mats. IONIQ 5 seat yarn uses recycled PET, about 32 bottles per car. — [Hyundai PL – Healthy Seas](https://www.hyundai.com/pl/o-hyundai/o-marce/wizja-zrownowazonej-przyszlosci/healthy-seas.html); [Autovista24](https://autovista24.autovistagroup.com/?p=31149); [Hyundai EU "Generation IONIQ"](https://www.hyundai.news/eu/articles/press-releases/generation-ioniq-sustainable-mobility-era.html)
- Hyundai also commissioned a Discovery documentary series on its circular-economy approach (content-series touchpoint; only the title was seen). — [Hyundai DE news](https://www.hyundai.news/de/articles/press-releases/discovery-documentary-series-takes-viewers-inside-hyundais-circular-economy-approach.html)

**E. NIO Life "Blue Sky Lab" (upcycled merchandise + physical retail in NIO House)**
- Blue Sky Lab is NIO Life's sustainable fashion label. It turns leftover automotive materials (seatbelts, airbags, car-grade fabrics) into fashion. — [Dezeen](https://www.dezeen.com/?p=1981025)
- Its first retail space sits in an airport NIO House next to the lounge. Pedestals display the recycled car by-products used to make the collections, and seatbelts double as clothing racks in the fit-out. The material story is told by the store fixtures themselves. — [Dezeen](https://www.dezeen.com/?p=1960911)
- Dezeen Awards 2023, sustainable design (consumer): one Dezeen page says shortlisted, another says longlisted, so the status is unresolved. — [Dezeen 1](https://www.dezeen.com/?p=1981025); [Dezeen 2](https://www.dezeen.com/?p=1960911)

**F. CUPRA x Seaqual and City Garages (material story + physical brand space)**
- CUPRA's partnership with Seaqual turns ocean clean-up waste into design materials. — [CUPRA newsroom](https://www.cupra.com/en-ie/newsroom/cupra-and-seaqual-turning-ocean-clean-up-into-innovative-design)
- City Garages use essential, natural materials such as concrete and copper, and recycled materials are said to be "prioritised wherever possible". This comes from an interview about the Berlin location. — [Sleek Mag](https://www.sleek-mag.com/article/a-visit-to-cupra-city-garage/)

**J. Polestar "No Compromises" (Super Bowl 2022: confrontational anti-greenwashing positioning)**
- A 30-second spot defining the brand by what it is not, including the line "no greenwashing". It was read as aimed at Tesla ("conquering Mars") and VW ("Dieselgate"). — [Polestar US](https://www.polestar.com/us/news/no-compromises-polestar-at-the-big-game/); [Business Insider NL](https://businessinsider.nl/electric-vehicle-firm-polestar-roasted-tesla-ceo-elon-musk-and-volkswagen-in-its-super-bowl-ad-with-references-to-conquering-mars-and-dieselgate/); [Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/brand-marketing/first-super-bowl-ad-polestar-elon-musk-greta-thunberg/)

### Inferences
- The best executions follow one pattern: **one programme → one tangible artefact or voice → one QR or data link**. Examples: trees that talk, a trunk liner with a QR code, seatbelts used as clothes racks. This is more distinctive than generic "x% recycled" claims.
- Hyundai shows that sustainability communication can win at Cannes without being about the car (biodiversity, forests, frogs). The link to the product is indirect, through the IONIQ sub-brand name (IONIQ Forest).

## Q2. How is it linked to the brand's business and product strategy?

### Takeaway
The links range from direct (Kia: an accessory sold for a new EV model; CUPRA: Tribe Editions as a product line; NIO: Blue Sky Lab as a NIO Life revenue line) to brand-halo (Hyundai: CSR forests carrying the IONIQ EV sub-brand name).

### Cited Findings
- The Kia liner is model-specific (EV3) and sold through dealers as an accessory. Sustainability becomes a sellable product at the point of sale, and the partnership feeds back into the EV launch. — [Kia EU press](https://prod2-press.kia.com/eu/en/home/media-resouces/press-releases/2024/kia-ocean-clean-up.html); [Lloyd Motor Group](https://www.lloydmotorgroup.com/Kia/Current-Offers/The-Ocean-Clean-Up-Trunk-Liner/628)
- Tree Correspondents was Hyundai's "first brand campaign on IONIQ Forest", a multi-year CSR programme on biodiversity and climate. — [Hyundai Motor Group](https://www.hyundaimotorgroup.com/en/news/hyundai-motor-wins-grand-prix-and-two-gold-lions-at-cannes-lions-with-night-fishing-and-tree-correspondents)
- Hyundai's 2026 release ties the award wins to its continuing commitment to zero-emission vehicles (FCEV and EV). — [Hyundai newsroom 2026](https://www.hyundai.com/worldwide/en/newsroom/detail/0000001210)
- CUPRA framed its Tribe Editions as blending "rebellious character with sustainable innovation", launched alongside the IAA 2025 brand moment. — [CUPRA media center](https://www.seat-cupra-mediacenter.com/CUPRA-Brand/cupra-news/2025/cupra-accelerates-growth-journey-with-tindaya-world-premiere-new-tribe-editions-and-middle-east-ambitions)
- Volvo's EX30 recognition rests on the car having been designed from the outset for the lowest carbon footprint of any Volvo. — [Cision](https://news.cision.com/volvo-car-uk/r/ground-breaking-volvo-ex30-honoured-in-the-2024-marie-claire-uk-sustainability-awards,c4194088)
- Renault's award-winning work in this period is about social sustainability and EV safety rather than environmental storytelling:
  - "Cars to Work" (free cars for unemployed people going to job interviews) won the SDG Grand Prix in 2024. — [Contagious](https://www.contagious.com/news-and-views/cannes-lions-2024-sustainable-development-goals); [CB News](https://www.cbnews.fr/node/85608)
  - "UN.patent" (opening the "Fireman Access" battery patent so EV battery fires can be put out in under 10 minutes) won Gold in Creative B2B in 2025. — [CB News](https://www.cbnews.fr/node/93630)

### Inferences
- The strongest business linkage is the "accessory" or "special edition" model (Kia liner, CUPRA Tribe): the sustainability story generates revenue and puts the narrative in the showroom.
- For Stellantis, Renault's "UN.patent" shows that sharing technology openly can be turned into award-winning communication. This could matter for Stellantis platform or battery know-how, or for SUSTAINera remanufacturing.

### Gaps
- No sales or uptake data was found for the Kia liner or for Blue Sky Lab.

## Q3. How is it adapted across markets and stakeholders?

### Takeaway
Hyundai localises one global platform through local ecosystems: trees in Brazil, Czechia and Korea; seaweed in Korea, Argentina and Australia; the coquí frog in Puerto Rico. Kia rolls out its accessory by region (Europe first).

### Cited Findings
- Tree sensors are in Brazil, the Czech Republic and Korea, and IONIQ Forest spans 13 countries. — [Muse by Clio](https://musebyclios.com/musings/how-fcb-n-y-used-ai-and-data-to-give-trees-a-voice/); [CleanTechnica](https://cleantechnica.com/2025/07/23/hyundai-motor-celebrates-10-years-of-ioniq-forest-with-tree-correspondents-campaign/)
- Forests Without Names covers Korea, Argentina and Australia, with the data shared with an NGO network (scientific stakeholders). — [Hyundai newsroom](https://www.hyundai.com/worldwide/en/newsroom/detail/0000001210)
- Coquí Alarmed was a market-specific execution (Puerto Rico) delivered through rental-car B2B partners to reach tourists. — [Hyundai newsroom](https://www.hyundai.com/worldwide/en/newsroom/detail/0000001210)
- The Kia liner is sold in select regions, including Europe. — [Kia EU press](https://prod2-press.kia.com/eu/en/home/media-resouces/press-releases/2024/kia-ocean-clean-up.html)
- Hyundai's Healthy Seas activity includes educational events for schoolchildren (community stakeholders) and is run by national Hyundai organisations (for example, the Polish site). — [Hyundai PL](https://www.hyundai.com/pl/o-hyundai/o-marce/wizja-zrownowazonej-przyszlosci/healthy-seas.html)
- NIO places Blue Sky Lab in an airport NIO House, aimed at premium travellers and users. — [Dezeen](https://www.dezeen.com/?p=1960911)

### Inferences
- A "global idea + local ecosystem" model (one platform, a different local species or site per market) suits Stellantis' multi-brand, multi-region footprint. Examples: Jeep with North American trails and parks, Fiat with Italian or Brazilian communities, Peugeot and Citroën in France and Europe.

### Gaps
- No evidence was found of differentiated messaging for investors or employees at these touchpoints.

## Q5. What could Stellantis brands concretely replicate?

### Takeaway
There are four transferable templates: (1) a QR-coded circular accessory sold at the dealer (Kia liner); (2) an upcycled lifestyle line sold in brand spaces (NIO Blue Sky Lab); (3) a product sound or feature hack localised to one market (Hyundai Coquí); (4) a data-driven "voice of nature" programme tied to a sub-brand (Hyundai Tree Correspondents / IONIQ Forest).

### Cited Findings
- Templates are based on the cases cited in Q1-Q4 (see URLs there).

### Inferences (proposals; none of these exist)
- **Jeep:** a Tree Correspondents-style programme, with live-sensor "trail voices" from protected areas Jeep supports or where it owns trails, carried by the 4xe sub-brand. Analogue: [Hyundai Tree Correspondents](https://musebyclios.com/musings/how-fcb-n-y-used-ai-and-data-to-give-trees-a-voice/).
- **Fiat (Grande Panda / 500e):** an accessory or merchandise line from SUSTAINera end-of-life materials, sold in dealers and online with a QR code to the material's origin. Analogues: [Kia liner](https://prod2-press.kia.com/eu/en/home/media-resouces/press-releases/2024/kia-ocean-clean-up.html) and [NIO Blue Sky Lab](https://www.dezeen.com/?p=1960911). Fiat's Italian design and fashion cachet suits a Milan Design Week launch.
- **Citroën:** a localised product hack (sound, light, or HMI feature) in a specific market, delivered through B2B fleets or rental partners, as in [Coquí Alarmed](https://www.hyundai.com/worldwide/en/newsroom/detail/0000001210).
- **Peugeot / DS:** a "Tribe Edition"-style special series with quantified recycled content as a showroom and configurator touchpoint, as in [CUPRA Tribe Editions](https://www.seat-cupra-mediacenter.com/CUPRA-Brand/cupra-news/2025/cupra-accelerates-growth-journey-with-tindaya-world-premiere-new-tribe-editions-and-middle-east-ambitions).
- **Stellantis/SUSTAINera:** an open-technology gesture, Renault "UN.patent" style, such as open remanufacturing or repair know-how. Analogue: [CB News](https://www.cbnews.fr/node/93630).
- **Risk guardrail:** avoid absolute "no greenwashing" claims (Polestar) and quantify recycled-content claims exactly (CUPRA forum vs official figures). With net zero moved to 2050, credibility at the touchpoint matters more than ambition.

### Gaps
- No checks were made of existing Stellantis brand touchpoints (for example Fiat or Peugeot recycled-material editions, Jeep trail programmes) to confirm what is already in place.
