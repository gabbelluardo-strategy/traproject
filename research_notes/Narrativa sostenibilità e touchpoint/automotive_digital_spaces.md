# Automotive sustainability communication via DIGITAL touchpoints and brand-owned PHYSICAL SPACES (benchmark for Stellantis, as of Oct 2026)

Scope note: excludes cases covered in the previous round (Hyundai Tree Correspondents/Coquí/IONIQ Forest, Kia x Ocean Cleanup, NIO Blue Sky Lab, CUPRA Seaqual/City Garage, Volvo EX30 interior, Polestar label, Volvo battery passport, BMW My BMW carbon report, BMW Debrecen, Renault Refactory, VW Gläserne Manufaktur). Direct fetch of company domains failed (rivian.com: DNS blocked), so all evidence comes from WebSearch over press releases, trade press and brand newsroom mirrors. Selected 8 cases: 5 digital, 4 physical (one digital case is weak and flagged).

## (A) Digital touchpoints: what is shown, how, strategy link, evidence, risk, transfer

### Takeaway
The most distinctive digital executions in 2024-2026 do not show "eco scores". They turn sustainability into a money metric the owner can feel: "charge/drive for free" V2G bundles (Renault/Mobilize in France, Octopus x BYD in the UK, Ford x Octopus in Germany from summer 2026). Gamified eco-coaching (Mercedes Eco Coach) is a warning case. After vouchers were cut in 2023, its points could only buy a "climate-protection contribution", the offset-based mechanic that EmpCo now restricts. Annual renewable-matching claims (Rivian) need careful wording after 27 Sept 2026.

### Cited Findings

**Case A1 - Renault 5 E-Tech + Mobilize V2G (France; first commercial V2G offer in Europe)**
- Launched October 2024 in France by Renault Group, Mobilize and The Mobility House. The pitch was that Renault 5 owners "can charge and drive for free" thanks to a bidirectional AC charger plus an energy contract. — [The Mobility House newsroom](https://www.mobilityhouse.com/int_en/our-company/newsroom/article/charge-for-free-renault-group-mobilize-and-the-mobility-house-launch-vehicle-to-grid-in-france-while-germany-is-establishing-the-regulatory-framework); [newmobility.news](https://newmobility.news/2024/10/23/france-first-to-launch-commercial-v2g-with-electric-renault-5/); [electrive](https://www.electrive.com/2024/10/22/renault-and-the-mobility-house-launch-v2g-project-in-france)
- What the owner gets: the Mobilize PowerBox Verso bidirectional wallbox (EUR 2,143 vs EUR 1,499 for the standard PowerBox Uno) and the "Mobilize Power" contract. At launch it was priced at EUR 0.2018/kWh ("22% lower" than the French regulated TRV, fixed for one year). V2G earnings are deducted from the electricity bill. — [newmobility.news](https://newmobility.news/2024/10/23/france-first-to-launch-commercial-v2g-with-electric-renault-5/)
- Brand claim: V2G income "could cover up to 50% of the cost of home charging" in France. This is a company projection. — [Renault media](https://media.renault.com/?p=182506); [Mobilize media](https://media.mobilize.com/?p=69604)
- Mechanism: the charger decides automatically whether to charge or export, based on battery needs, household needs and grid/market incentives. The contract with The Mobility House "guarantees green electricity". — [electricdrives.tv](https://electricdrives.tv/ev-biz/the-renault-5-pioneering-vehicle-to-grid-technology/); [The Mobility House](https://www.mobilityhouse.com/int_en/our-company/newsroom/article/charge-for-free-renault-group-mobilize-and-the-mobility-house-launch-vehicle-to-grid-in-france-while-germany-is-establishing-the-regulatory-framework)
- Market adaptation: the original 2023 plan was France and Germany in 2024 and the UK in 2025. At the France launch, Germany was described as "still establishing the regulatory framework". — [electrive.net 2023](https://www.electrive.net/2023/06/13/renault-fuehrt-mit-elektro-5er-bidirektionales-laden-ein/); [The Mobility House](https://www.mobilityhouse.com/int_en/our-company/newsroom/article/charge-for-free-renault-group-mobilize-and-the-mobility-house-launch-vehicle-to-grid-in-france-while-germany-is-establishing-the-regulatory-framework)

**Case A2 - Octopus Energy x BYD "Power Pack" bundle (UK, 2025) and Ford x Octopus V2G (Germany, summer 2026)**
- The BYD bundle was announced June 2025 as the UK's first V2G car bundle: a leased BYD Dolphin, a Zaptec Pro bidirectional charger and an Octopus smart tariff for about GBP 299 per month ("under GBP 300" in some sources). It promises free home charging for every mile driven during the lease. — [Fleet News](https://www.fleetnews.co.uk/news/octopus-and-byd-to-offer-uk-first-vehicle-to-grid-bundle-with-free-ev-charging); [The Energyst](https://theenergyst.com/octopus-byd-launch-v2g-car-and-charging-bundle/)
- Conditions (single source): plug in at least 20 times a month for at least 12 hours each, within a kWh cap tied to lease mileage. Octopus claims about GBP 620/year savings vs a standard tariff, which is its own estimate. — [evpowered.co.uk](https://evpowered.co.uk/news/octopus-and-byd-launch-uks-first-v2g-car-bundle-with-free-ev-charging/); [brokernews](https://brokernews.co.uk/octopus-and-byd-offer-uks-first-v2g-power-pack-bundle-with-free-charging/)
- Inclusion angle: partnered with Motability Operations to offer V2G to disabled drivers. — [Fleet News](https://www.fleetnews.co.uk/news/octopus-and-byd-to-offer-uk-first-vehicle-to-grid-bundle-with-free-ev-charging)
- A 2026 UK buyer's guide still lists the BYD Dolphin V2G bundle at about GBP 300/month. — [bestchargers.co.uk](https://www.bestchargers.co.uk/electric-cars/vehicles-compatible-with-v2g-charging/)
- Ford x Octopus (Germany), announced Feb 2026 for summer 2026, covers MY2026 Ford Capri and Explorer only. It bundles an Octopus V2G tariff, vehicle activation and an Ambibox bidirectional DC wallbox. Benefits: a EUR 30/month bonus if the car is plugged in at least 300 h/month (max EUR 360/year), plus an 18 ct/kWh discount on the charging share (effective about 15 ct/kWh at a 33 ct household price). Ford headlines this as "up to 16,000 km free per year", a theoretical figure. — [electrive.net](https://www.electrive.net/2026/02/09/ford-legt-den-v2g-schalter-fuer-privat-um-gekoppelt-mit-tarif-von-octopus-energy/); [emove360](https://www.emove360.com/ford-and-octopus-energy-launch-bidirectional-charging-up-to-16000-kilometers-possible-free-of-charge/); [insideevs.de](https://insideevs.de/news/786717/ford-vehicle2grid-octopus-energy/)
- Context: BMW also launched a German V2G offer around the same time. — [autozeitung](https://www.autozeitung.de/bidirektionales-laden-ford-bmw-v2g-angebote-news-300505.html). Octopus launched an all-in-one EV lease + charging + home energy offer in Germany in Sept 2025. — [electrive](https://www.electrive.com/2025/09/11/octopus-launches-all-in-one-ev-leasing-charging-and-home-energy-offer-in-germany)

**Case A3 - Mercedes-Benz "Eco Coach" app (gamified charging and battery-care coaching), with a compliance warning**
- What it does: combines information with gamification "to improve the long-term performance of the battery" for PHEV/EV owners. Users earn points by completing challenges such as maximising the share of a journey driven electrically, charging at different locations, or charging on consecutive days (for example, 100 points for "charge in three different locations"). It also runs monthly community competitions. — [Mercedes-Benz Group](https://group.mercedes-benz.com/innovation/digitalisation/mobility-services/eco-coach-app.html); [emobilityplus](https://emobilityplus.com/2021/01/19/mercedes-me-eco-coach-a-personal-trainer-for-eco-friendly-driving/)
- Rewards at launch (Germany, 2021): charging vouchers for Mercedes me Charge, Mercedes Collection vouchers, or CO2 compensation. Markets: DE, UK, FR, ES, PT, BE, NL, SE, DK. — [emobilityplus](https://emobilityplus.com/2021/01/19/mercedes-me-eco-coach-a-personal-trainer-for-eco-friendly-driving/); [visor.ph](https://visor.ph/industry/this-mercedes-benz-phone-app-makes-eco-driving-fun/)
- Evidence of uptake: a German outlet headlined "Mercedes me Eco Coach celebrates 50,000 app users" (with discounted charging vouchers). — [meinauto.de](https://www.meinauto.de/news/ermasigte-ladegutscheine-mercedes-me-eco-coach-feiert-50-000-app-user)
- Partly discontinued: from July 2023, points could no longer be converted into charging vouchers. They can only go to a "climate-protection contribution" (offset-type), though existing vouchers stay redeemable. — [mbpassion.de](https://mbpassion.de/2023/07/ladegutscheine-ueber-eco-coach-app-eingestellt/); [jesmb.de](https://jesmb.de/17761/)
- The service was still listed by Mercedes in a page updated June 2025. No evidence of full shutdown was found. — [Mercedes-Benz Group](https://group.mercedes-benz.com/technology/digitalisation/digital-services/eco-coach-app.html)

**Case A4 - Rivian Adventure Network and Waypoints: "100% renewable" charging claim (US/Canada)**
- Rivian says the electricity used at its RAN and Waypoints chargers is matched with renewable energy on an annual basis, through PPAs, onsite and community solar/wind and utility partnerships. In fossil-heavy grids it says it works to add renewables locally. — [Business Wire 2022](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20220627005519/en); [Rivian Support](https://rivian.com/en-CA/support/article/how-does-rivian-power-its-charging-networks-with-100-renewable-energy) (fetch blocked; content via search snippet)
- Next-generation RAN sites (first at Joshua Tree, CA) repeat the 100% matching claim. — [The EV Report](https://theevreport.com/rivian-expands-nationwide-charging-access)
- It is an annual-matching accounting claim, not hourly or real-time, and is self-reported with no independent verification found. — same sources

**Case A5 - Polestar: per-variant LCA published (configurator per-option display not confirmed)**
- Polestar publishes per-variant production footprints. For the Polestar 4: Standard range Single motor 19.4 tCO2e, Long range Single motor 19.9, Long range Dual motor 21.4. Battery modules are 36-40% of materials and refining emissions; aluminium is 23-24%. Lifetime footprint is 37 t with the global electricity mix vs 23 t with wind-only charging. ISO 14044 method, 200,000 km over 15 years. — [The Driven](https://thedriven.io/2023/11/01/polestar-4-to-boast-lowest-carbon-footprint-of-all-polestar-evs-despite-huge-battery); [Polestar 2 carbon footprint summary PDF, Mar 2026](https://www.polestar.com/dato-assets/11286/1772704325-polestar-2_carbon-footprint_summary_260304.pdf)
- No evidence was found that any OEM configurator in scope (Polestar, BMW, Mercedes, Peugeot, Skoda, Lucid, Rivian) prices each option in kg CO2e. — searches returned only PDFs/LCAs.

### Inferences
- **Strongest pattern for engagement: "free kilometres" beats "saved CO2".** All three V2G offers lead with money and mobility ("charge/drive for free", "16,000 km free", "GBP 620/year"). The environmental benefit (grid balancing, absorbing renewables) is implicit. This is simple and visual, and it avoids generic eco claims.
- **The V2G offers sell a bundle, not a car.** Car + wallbox + tariff are sold as one subscription-like product. This supports EV take-up, which is directly relevant to Stellantis' FaSTLAne 2030 BEV mix and to Free2Move/energy partners.
- **EmpCo risk is concrete:**
  - Mercedes Eco Coach, where points convert only to a "climate-protection contribution", is exactly the offset-based mechanic EmpCo constrains.
  - Rivian's "100% renewable" via annual matching would need precise, substantiated wording in the EU. A claim like "charging is 100% green" without explaining matching is high-risk.
  - "Drive for free" is a pricing claim, not an environmental one, but it must still meet unfair-commercial-practice rules (conditions such as 20 plug-ins/month or 300 h/month must be clear).
- **Transfer ideas for Stellantis brands (inference):**
  - Fiat Grande Panda / 500e or Citroën ë-C3: a "km offerti dalla rete" (kilometres paid for by the grid) bundle with an energy partner, shown in the app as one counter: "km guadagnati questo mese" (km earned this month), with no CO2 claim.
  - Peugeot e-208/e-3008: rebuild an Eco Coach-style challenge layer (battery care, off-peak charging) whose rewards are real benefits (charging credit, service discount), never offsets.
  - Jeep 4xe: a challenge for the share of electric km on PHEVs. Plug-in rates are a known PHEV credibility issue, so this would show actual e-km share, an EmpCo-safe factual metric.

### Gaps
- No 2025-2026 update was found on Mobilize V2G customer numbers, the Germany/UK rollout, or current tariff terms. The EUR 0.2018/kWh and 50% figures are from 2024.
- Octopus x BYD uptake figures were not found. The "Power Pack" app UI (screens showing earnings) was not documented.
- Not researched for lack of budget, so no verified data: Hyundai/Kia eco score and Green Zone, Toyota hybrid coaching score, Nissan Carwings ranking (Carwings was legacy and superseded by NissanConnect, so likely discontinued, unverified), Ford SmartGauge, BMW eDrive Zones, VW We Charge green power, Tesla energy app, gaming/virtual experiences.

## (B) Brand-owned physical spaces: what is shown, how, strategy link, evidence, risk, transfer

### Takeaway
Sustainability stories that work in physical spaces turn materials into something tangible and hands-on rather than relying on text panels. Examples: Hyundai Motorstudio Busan's "Plastic" show with the Vitra Design Museum and recycled-part touch points, Motorstudio upcycling workshops, Audi's House of Progress reusable pavilion and circular-design programme at Milan Design Week, and NIO House as a certified-building plus community hub. Evidence of effectiveness (visitor counts) is thin in public sources.

### Cited Findings

**Case B1 - Hyundai Motorstudio (Busan/Seoul/Jakarta): curated exhibition + upcycling workshops**
- "Plastic: Remaking Our World" with the Vitra Design Museum opened at Hyundai Motorstudio Busan on 28 Aug 2024. Visitors could touch recycled parts used in IONIQ 5/IONIQ 6 and learn about Hyundai's plastic-to-hydrogen technology. A workshop zone let visitors exchange plastic bottle caps for recycled souvenirs. The installation "Plastic Atlas" (by Yeon) was made from Hyundai's recycled fabric: seat covers, airbags and seatbelts from end-of-life vehicles. — [Hyundai Worldwide newsroom](https://www.hyundai.com/worldwide/en/newsroom/detail/0000000814); [Hyundai Motor Group](https://www.hyundaimotorgroup.com/en/news/CONT0000000000159825); [hyundai.news EU](https://www.hyundai.news/eu/articles/press-releases/hyundai-motor-and-vitra-design-museum-open-exhibition.html)

### Inferences
- **Hands-on material proof works better than claims.** Touching a recycled part, trading a bottle cap for an object, or making a keyring from scrap leather is tangible and specific. These are reductions and recycled-content facts, which are EmpCo-safe if quantified.
- **The building can be the message.** Audi's reusable pavilion and NIO's certified building show a principle without slogans. Caveat: NIO's "carbon-neutral" building description is the kind of neutrality wording that is risky in EU communication if it relies on offsets. Under EmpCo it is safer to state the solar share or certification level.
- **Partnering with a design institution adds credibility and reach.** Vitra (Hyundai) and Milan Design Week (Audi) give credibility and earned media.
- **Transfer ideas for Stellantis (inference):**
  - Fiat / Lancia showrooms or Casa Fiat-type spaces in Torino (e.g., Lingotto/Mirafiori): a SUSTAINera "touch wall" of remanufactured parts with price and kg-of-material-saved labels, plus a Hyundai-style workshop (bottle-caps or scrap-to-object) built on SUSTAINera reman/reuse streams.
  - Alfa Romeo / DS: a Milan Design Week House-of-Progress-style circular-design installation.
  - Peugeot: zoning modelled on Kia360's EV Life Zone for an "electric life" corner in flagship stores.

## Regulatory filter: EU Empowering Consumers Directive (EmpCo, 2024/825)

### Takeaway
EmpCo has applied since 27 Sept 2026. It bans generic environmental claims, offset-based "neutral/reduced/positive impact" claims and unverified sustainability labels, including for products already on the market. That makes offset-based rewards (Mercedes Eco Coach) and loose "100% green / carbon-neutral" wording (Rivian, NIO building) the main risks in the cases above. Money/km benefits and quantified recycled content are the safer route.

### Cited Findings
- EmpCo rules apply across the EU from 27 Sept 2026. The Commission has published a Q&A. — [European Outdoor Group summary of Commission Q&A](https://www.europeanoutdoorgroup.com/members-knowledge-hub/commission-updated-the-q-a-on-the-empowering-consumers-for-the-green-transition-directive); [TLT](https://www.tlt.com/insights-and-events/insight/the-eu-is-empowering-consumers-for-the-green-transition-directive-what-your-business-needs-to-do-before-september-2026)
- Bans claims of neutral, reduced or positive GHG impact based on offsetting. Sustainability labels need third-party certification schemes, and unverified labels must be removed. Applies to all traders selling to EU consumers. — [osapiens](https://osapiens.com/blog/empco-fact-check/); [McCann FitzGerald](https://www.mccannfitzgerald.com/knowledge/disputes/the-european-union-empowering-consumers-for-the-green-transition-regulations-2026-key-implications-for-businesses); [Anthesis](https://www.anthesisgroup.com/?p=73828)
- EmpCo is distinct from the Green Claims Directive, which was still not adopted as of mid-2026. — [osapiens](https://osapiens.com/blog/empco-fact-check/)
- Taylor Wessing published an automotive-specific part of its green-claims series. Content was not retrieved. — [Taylor Wessing](https://www.taylorwessing.com/en/insights-and-events/insights/2025/03/navigating-the-future-of-green-claims)

### Inferences
- **Usable EU claim types:** specific, measurable, product-level facts (kg recycled material, % renewable at a named site with method stated, kWh shifted or exported by V2G) and economic benefits.
- **Avoid:** "climate-neutral" rewards, points-for-offsets, and unqualified "green charging".
- **Fine figure is unconfirmed:** a penalty of "at least 4% of turnover / EUR 2m" appeared in one search summary but its source was not confirmed, so it is treated as unverified.

### Gaps
- Official Journal text and the automotive-specific guidance were not read directly. National transposition status per member state was not checked.
