# Orchestrating sustainability communication as a system: one narrative, many touchpoints (multi-brand groups, physical/corporate touchpoints, employees, market localization)

Research notes for the Stellantis benchmark (ESG + HR Communication), current as of 14 Oct 2026. Method note: direct WebFetch of company domains was blocked (e.g. `loreal.com`: getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND), so findings come from WebSearch results. Every URL below came back in search results; the claims are from search snippets and summaries, not full-page reads. Treat company-reported figures as self-reported. No direct visual URLs could be verified (no fetch), so visuals are limited to case pages that likely contain hero images.

## Q1. Multi-brand groups: how is a group-level narrative carried into each brand's voice while staying coherent (shared labels, design systems, icons)?

### Takeaway
The strongest multi-brand pattern is not a shared slogan. It is a **shared, group-owned measurement and labelling tool that every brand displays in its own voice**. L'Oréal's A–E Product Impact Labelling is the reference case: one methodology, verified by Bureau Veritas, rolled out brand by brand (Garnier first) and country by country. That approach now faces a hard regulatory test in the EU: since 27 Sept 2026, self-developed sustainability labels are barred unless they are third-party verified. Inditex dropping "Join Life" (2023) is the cautionary counter-case. Polestar (Geely group) shows the automotive version: a product sustainability declaration on every car.

### Cited Findings
**L'Oréal: Product Environmental & Social Impact Labelling (group tool, brand-by-brand rollout)**
- Each product gets an A–E score (A = best). The score draws on **14 planetary impact factors**, including GHG, water scarcity, ocean acidification and biodiversity. The method was designed under L'Oréal's 10-year sustainability plan and endorsed by independent experts, with data verified by independent auditors — [Circular Online](https://www.circularonline.co.uk/news/loreal-rolls-out-new-environmental-labelling-methodology/)
- Rollout sequence: France 2020 on **Garnier** hair care first; then the US in 2022 (Kiehl's, L'Oréal Paris, Redken, CeraVe to follow). Garnier had ranked 100+ hair-care products, 90% at "B" or better — [CosmeticsDesign (US rollout, Dec 2022)](https://cosmeticsdesign.com/Article/2022/12/15/L-Oreal-USA-Garnier-launches-Product-Impact-labelling-initiative-in-the-US-for-scores-on-environmental-social-impacts); [CosmeticsDesign-Europe (Garnier France 2020)](https://www.cosmeticsdesign-europe.com/Article/2020/06/30/L-Oreal-s-Garnier-rolls-out-sustainability-impact-scores-on-hair-care-in-France)
- Canada 2023: Garnier, La Roche-Posay and Biotherm went first, on their websites from April 2023 and then gradually on pack via **QR code**. The label also shows social info: compliance with UN labour principles and the number of suppliers committed to social inclusion — [L'Oréal Canada newsroom](https://www.loreal.com/en/canada/news/news/l’oreal-canada-launches-product-impact-labeling-system/); [Canadian Grocer](https://canadiangrocer.com/loreal-canada-rolling-out-product-labelling-system-improve-transparency)
- Bureau Veritas certified the application of the methodology and the accuracy of the data — [ESG Today](https://www.esgtoday.com/?p=11109)
- Status gap: no 2025–2026 source on current global brand and country coverage turned up. The official page could not be fetched: https://www.loreal.com/en/commitments-and-responsibilities/for-the-planet/product-environmental-and-social-impact-labelling/

**Inditex "Join Life": discontinued (flag)**
- Inditex discontinued the Join Life indicator on garments, disclosed in its annual report published March 2023. Its stated reason: "no longer necessary to differentiate the products in our collections with this label." The label had covered 61% of products. Join Life is kept as an **internal standard**, and Inditex began working with Quantis on LCAs. Mango had dropped its "Committed" label at the end of 2022 — [Ecotextile](https://www.ecotextile.com/2023033146208/news/legislation/inditex-to-end-product-sustainability-label/); [FashionNetwork](https://ca.fashionnetwork.com/news/Inditex-discontinues-sustainability-label-join-life-,1500958.html); [TheIndustry.fashion](https://www.theindustry.fashion/inditex-discontinues-join-life-sustainability-indicator-as-it-is-deemed-no-longer-necessary/)
- The press linked the move to the coming EU anti-greenwashing rules. That link is the outlets' inference, not Inditex's statement — [Ecotextile](https://www.ecotextile.com/2023033146208/news/legislation/inditex-to-end-product-sustainability-label/)

**Polestar (Geely group): product sustainability declaration on every car**
- Each product carries a declaration showing **cradle-to-gate t CO2e and traced materials**, with recycled/renewable content and circularity to be added. It is published on the website and in Polestar retail "spaces". Polestar 2 came first: 26 t CO2e leaving the factory. The campaign line is "We do believe in labels" — [Polestar newsroom](https://www.polestar.com/global/news/we-do-believe-in-labels/); [Car Design News](https://www.cardesignnews.com/cars/polestars-sustainability-mission-becomes-all-encompassing/442095)
- It sits inside the "Polestar 0" project (truly climate-neutral car by 2030, explicitly **without offsetting**) — [Polestar: striving for zero](https://www.polestar.com/en-sg/news/striving-for-zero-the-2030-climate-neutral-car-plan/); [NewMobility](https://newmobility.news/2021/04/08/polestar-we-want-a-truly-climate-neutral-car-by-2030/)
- No result covered whether Volvo Cars, Zeekr or Lotus use the same declaration format. So there is **no evidence of a Geely group-wide shared label**.

**LVMH: LIFE 360 group framework, Maison-level execution**
- LIFE 360 is the group framework. The **LIFE Academy** trains employees, and its first campus opened at La Millière near Paris in 2024. The target is to train all employees on environmental issues by 2026. There are two tracks (fundamentals vs. expert: eco-design, responsible sourcing, climate) and courses tailored to each business group since 2023 — [LVMH LIFE Academy](https://lvmh.com/commitment-in-action/for-the-environment/life-academy); [LVMH campus news](https://www.lvmh.com/en/news-lvmh/lvmh-and-association-de-la-vallee-de-la-milliere-open-the-first-campus-dedicated-to-environmental-training-for-group-employees)
- In 2025, **25,000+ employees** completed environmental / Green IT modules. Progress is tracked across **75 Maisons** through about **200 environmental correspondents**, a group-to-brand relay network. Selected programmes are opened to value-chain partners — [LVMH: for the environment](https://www.lvmh.com/en/commitment-in-action/for-the-environment); [LVMH supporting stakeholders](https://www.lvmh.com/en/commitment-in-action/for-the-environment/accompagner-les-parties-prenantes)
- The "LIFE in Stores" awards initiative brings the framework to retail touchpoints — [LVMH LIFE in Stores](https://www.lvmh.com/group/lvmh-commitments/social-environmental-responsibility/life-in-stores-awards-initiative-lvmh/)

**Hyundai Motor: brand vision "Progress for Humanity" turned into award-winning executions**
- "The Bigger Crash" (2022) used print and outdoor photos of cars overturned by hurricanes, captioned with storm wind speeds instead of vehicle speeds. It won Cannes Silver Lions (Outdoor, Print), a D&AD Graphite Pencil and 7 One Show awards. Hyundai explicitly tied it to the brand vision "Progress for Humanity" — [Hyundai Worldwide newsroom](https://www.hyundai.com/worldwide/en/newsroom/detail/0000000079); [Hyundai EU news](https://www.hyundai.news/eu/articles/press-releases/the-bigger-crash-brand-campaign-ads-win-silver-lions-2022.html)
- "Tree Correspondents" (2025, FCB New York) put sensors on IONIQ Forest trees and used an AI language model to turn real-time environmental data into first-person "tree" posts. The trees took over Hyundai's Threads account, backed by a documentary film. It launched 21 Mar 2025 (International Day of Forests), marking 10 years and **1 million trees** of IONIQ Forest. It won **2 Gold + 1 Silver Lion (Digital Craft)** at Cannes 2025 — [Hyundai Brand Journal](https://www.hyundai.com/worldwide/en/brand-journal/sustainable-vision/tree-correspondents-campaign-cannes); [Hyundai Motor Group news](https://www.hyundaimotorgroup.com/en/news/CONT0000000000181663); [CleanTechnica](https://cleantechnica.com/2025/07/23/hyundai-motor-celebrates-10-years-of-ioniq-forest-with-tree-correspondents-campaign/); [Ads of the World](https://www.adsoftheworld.com/campaigns/tree-correspondents); [PR Newswire PDF on 2025 Cannes wins](https://mma.prnewswire.com/media/2713650/Press_Release__Hyundai_Motor_Wins_Grand_Prix_and_Two_Gold_Lions_at_Cannes_Lions_0618_updated.pdf?p=pdf)

### Inferences
- The scalable mechanism in multi-brand groups is a **"group engine, brand skin"** model: one group-owned, verified methodology (L'Oréal score, Polestar declaration) or group framework (LIFE 360). Each brand then shows it in its own design language and at its own pace, with pilot brands first (Garnier, Polestar 2).
- After 27 Sept 2026 (EmpCo), any Stellantis cross-brand "eco-badge" would have to be third-party verified, or it becomes illegal in the EU (see Q4). A **verified data declaration** (Polestar-style LCA disclosure) is safer than an **evaluative label** ("green", A–E). Inditex retreating to an internal standard is a model for keeping a label as an internal design-and-governance tool without consumer exposure.
- Hyundai shows how a long-running group programme (IONIQ Forest, 10 years) gives brand teams a permanent asset that can be re-skinned creatively each year. For Stellantis, Motor Citizens or SUSTAINera could play that role.
- **Proposal for Stellantis:** a "Stellantis Impact Sheet". It would be a group-owned, third-party-verified data module (cradle-to-gate CO2e, recycled content, SUSTAINera parts availability, plant renewable-energy share) that each of the 14 brands shows in its own visual identity (configurator, showroom QR, brand site). It would launch with one pilot brand and model in one market.

### Gaps
- Unilever, Kering, AB InBev, Diageo, Accor, VW Group brand-level and Geely group-wide executions were not verified in this pass. One third-party, unverified source said Unilever merged sustainability, comms and corporate affairs in Jan 2025 under CSO Rebecca Marmot ([imogenpr](https://imogenpr.com/?p=384)); confirm before use.
- No 2025–26 status of L'Oréal label coverage was found.
- No shared cross-brand icon set (design-system level) could be documented for any automotive group.

## Q2. Physical corporate touchpoints that tell the sustainability story (visitable plants, HQs, pavilions)

### Takeaway
Plants that work as stories can be powerful, but they are fragile. VW's Gläserne Manufaktur, the iconic "transparent factory", **ended vehicle production on 16 Dec 2025** and is becoming an innovation campus; visitor tours continue along the former line. BMW Debrecen ("first plant operating without fossil fuels", Neue Klasse iX3, Sept 2025) is the new automotive reference. Renault's Flins Refactory is the closest analogue to SUSTAINera: a former assembly plant re-narrated as a circular-economy site and toured by academics and executives. Schneider turns WEF Lighthouse status into a recurring visit programme.

### Cited Findings
**BMW Group Plant Debrecen (Hungary)**
- Opened Sept 2025, planned virtually under the BMW iFACTORY principles. It is the first BMW plant to rely entirely on renewable electricity in normal operation. Oliver Zipse called it "the first production site to operate entirely without fossil fuels". It has the first fossil-free paint shop in BMW's network (Power-to-Heat, heat grid) and an E.ON solar park sized for about 20,000 households. First model: BMW iX3 (Neue Klasse) — [Sustainability Magazine](https://sustainabilitymag.com/news/bmws-debrecen-plant-becomes-first-fossil-fuel-free-facility); [BMW Group PressClub Hungary attachment](https://www.press.bmwgroup.com/hungary/article/attachment/T0452987HU/638139); [BMWBlog 2022](https://www.bmwblog.com/2022/04/30/bmw-debrecen-plant-will-be-the-companys-first-carbon-neutral-factory)
- The narrative was reframed from "carbon neutral" (2022) to "fossil-free", which is likely more robust under EmpCo — [BMWBlog](https://www.bmwblog.com/2022/04/30/bmw-debrecen-plant-will-be-the-companys-first-carbon-neutral-factory) vs. [Sustainability Magazine](https://sustainabilitymag.com/news/bmws-debrecen-plant-becomes-first-fossil-fuel-free-facility). No visitor-programme details were found.

**VW Gläserne Manufaktur Dresden: production ended (flag)**
- Vehicle production ended 16 Dec 2025 after 24 years. VW, Saxony and TU Dresden signed a letter of intent for an innovation campus (AI, robotics, chip design): more than €50M and 4 endowed professorships over 7 years. Only an LOI exists so far. Visitor tours continue along the former line, along with daily EV test drives (ID.3/4/5/7, ID. Buzz). The site stays a delivery location and "world of experience", with about 3,500 deliveries in 2025 — [electrive 1 Dec 2025](https://www.electrive.com/2025/12/01/volkswagen-to-turn-dresden-factory-into-innovation-hub); [electrive 4 Dec 2025](https://www.electrive.com/2025/12/04/vw-confirms-innovation-centre-in-dresden); [Silicon Saxony](https://silicon-saxony.de/en/tu-dresden-and-vw-future-concept-in-place-innovation-campus-becomes-part-of-the-glaeserne-manufaktur-dresden/); [VW newsroom](https://www.volkswagen-newsroom.com/de/dresden-3730); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transparent_Factory)

**Renault Group Refactory, Flins (circular economy)**
- Presented as Europe's only plant dedicated to automotive circular economy. Its 4 pillars: Re-trofit (vehicle life extension), Re-energy (batteries, hydrogen), Re-cycle (parts), Re-start (3D printing, skills). Over 90% of the 1,938 employees were moved to reconditioning; the site had built cars since 1952. "The Future is Neutral" is the circular-economy company based there — [ESSEC Circular Economy Chair site visits](https://circular-economy-chair.essec.edu/our-program/site-visits); [Automotive Manufacturing Solutions](https://www.automotivemanufacturingsolutions.com/strategy/renault-advances-circular-economy-reuse-remake-recycle-business/532835); [electrive ES](https://www.electrive.com/es/2022/10/14/renault-creates-the-future-is-neutral-for-circular-economy/)
- It is used as a showcase for executives (Nissan COO visit, Sept 2022, framed as "emblematic of Renaulution") and for universities (UVSQ master students, a "campus des métiers" on circular mobility) — [Alliance RNM](https://alliancernm.com/2022/09/08/a-gupta-visits-the-renault-re-factory-flins/); [UVSQ](https://www.uvsq.fr/des-etudiants-de-luvsq-en-visite-a-la-re-factory-renault-de-flins?toPdf=true)
- Renault announced 150 new jobs at Flins during the conversion. The "jobs" framing anchors the sustainability story in the local community — [L'Info Durable](https://www.linfodurable.fr/renault-va-creer-150-emplois-sur-son-site-de-flins-en-pleine-reconversion-41853)

**Schneider Electric: WEF Lighthouse / Sustainability Lighthouse sites as visit programmes**
- Le Vaudreuil (a Sustainability Lighthouse) is toured by business school students (ESCP), who see digital tools behind −25% energy, −17% material waste and −25% CO2 — [ESCP](https://escp.eu/news/exploring-future-industry-and-innovation-escp-students-dive-schneider-electrics-smart-ecosystem)
- About 100 Manufacturing Leadership Council members toured the Monterrey, Mexico Lighthouse — [MLC](https://manufacturingleadershipcouncil.com/in-mexico-schneider-electric-showcases-lighthouse-accomplishments/)
- Evreux distribution centre: Schneider's 6th Sustainability Lighthouse. Hyderabad plant was also recognised — [The Manufacturer](https://www.themanufacturer.com/articles/schneiders-evreux-dc-named-sustainability-lighthouse-by-world-economic-forum/); [pv magazine India](https://www.pv-magazine-india.com/press-releases/world-economic-forum-recognizes-schneider-electrics-hyderabad-factory-as-a-sustainability-lighthouse/); [Sustainability Magazine](https://sustainabilitymag.com/news/schneiders-11-lighthouse-industrial-showcases)

### Inferences
- External certification (WEF Sustainability Lighthouse) gives a plant a **third-party "badge"** that holds up under green-claims scrutiny. That makes it a strong storytelling asset for B2B, talent and government audiences.
- Flins → **SUSTAINera** is the closest analogy. Stellantis could make one SUSTAINera hub (e.g. Mirafiori / Turin circular hub; verify) a "visitable proof point" with a standard guided route, aimed at employees, students, dealers and policymakers.
- VW Dresden shows the risk: a showcase plant tied to a product strategy (Phaeton, then ID.3) can lose its production role. The visitor and experience layer survived, which suggests **separating the "story venue" from the production function**.
- **Proposal:** a "Stellantis Proof Points Route". It would select 1 plant per region (Europe, North America, Brazil, MEA), each telling one verifiable fact (renewable share, SUSTAINera reman, Bio-Hybrid ethanol, etc.) with common wayfinding and a common visual grammar. It would also pursue WEF Lighthouse/Sustainability Lighthouse recognition where eligible.

### Gaps
- Mercedes Factory 56, Novo Nordisk, Interface, Enel/Ørsted/Bosch HQs and Expo/COP pavilions were not researched in this pass (tool budget).
- No visitor-number data was found for Debrecen, Refactory or Schneider Lighthouses.
- Current (2026) Refactory status was not verified.

## Q3. Employee touchpoints and employer branding (training, ambassadors, engagement, ownership)

### Takeaway
The distinctive employee programmes combine **structure** (a named academy or school with a universal training target), **a distributed relay network** (correspondents or ambassadors at every site), and **a stake** (ownership or voice). LVMH (25k+ trained, 200 correspondents across 75 Maisons), Schneider (Sustainability Ambassadors at every site, Act for Green site scoring, Sustainability School later opened externally) and Danone (One Person One Voice One Share) are the strongest. Danone's link between share ownership and sustainability voice maps directly onto Stellantis' **Shares to Win**.

### Cited Findings
**Schneider Electric**
- **Sustainability Ambassadors** are present at every Schneider location worldwide. **Act for Green** grew out of them: sites are benchmarked on a sustainability checklist, and ambassadors measure and drive score improvements — [The Safety Mag](https://www.thesafetymag.com/ca/topics/leadership-and-culture/schneider-electrics-act-for-green-program-engages-employees-on-sustainability/301739)
- Canada: green teams evolved into a "Site Sustainability Ambassador Program". Schneider Canada was named one of Canada's Greenest Employers 2025 — [Electrical Industry News](https://electricalindustry.ca/?p=81494)
- **Sustainability School**: first launched internally to train employees to support the partner ecosystem, later opened to external professionals and companies — [Schneider press release](https://www.se.com/ww/en/about-us/newsroom/news/press-releases/schneider-electric’s-first-sustainability-school-opens-for-enrolment-646b77fd7854e9e9160ca954); [3BL Media](https://www.3blmedia.com/news/schneider-electrics-first-sustainability-school-opens-enrolment)
- **Sustainability@Home** (since 2022) reached about 34,000 North American employees, encouraging greener homes, with a discount site for sustainable products — [HR Brew](https://www.hr-brew.com/stories/2023/11/14/how-schneider-electric-s-hr-team-encourages-employees-to-be-greener-at-home)

**LVMH LIFE Academy** (see Q1): 25k+ employees trained in 2025, ~200 environmental correspondents, 75 Maisons, physical campus at La Millière — [LVMH](https://lvmh.com/commitment-in-action/for-the-environment/life-academy)

**Danone "One Person, One Voice, One Share" (OPOVOS)**
- Launched April 2018 with the 2030 Goals. Each employee was granted one Danone share plus an annual dividend-based incentive. "One Voice" is an annual consultation in which every employee can submit ideas on the 2030 Goals, run on the WTW survey platform. The Board created a Purpose & Engagement Committee to oversee it — [GlobeNewswire 2018](https://www.globenewswire.com/de/news-release/2018/04/26/1488408/0/en/DANONE-Danone-sets-2030-Goals-for-long-term-sustainable-value-creation-supported-by-an-innovative-governance-and-employee-engagement-model.html); [WTW](https://wtwco.com/en-ae/insights/2021/09/how-employee-insights-power-danones-sustainable-growth-agenda); [AMF filing 2019](https://echanges.dila.gouv.fr/OPENDATA/AMF/MKW/2019/04/FCMKW119487_20190425.pdf)
- 86,000+ employees took part in the 2020 One Voice survey (case-study claim) — [ICMR case](https://icmrindia.org/Casestudies/catalogue/Human%20Resource%20and%20Organization%20Behavior/danones-one-person-case.htm)
- Danone's own governance turmoil (CEO Faber ousted in 2021) is a known risk of over-identifying the corporate narrative with purpose. No source was retrieved for this in this pass; treat it as context only.

**Renault Group / Climate Fresk**
- Renault Trucks (now Volvo Group, not Renault Group; caution) reported about 2,800 staff through the Climate Fresk with 50+ internal facilitators (doc dated July 2024) — [Renault Trucks Good Move PDF](https://renault-trucks.com/sites/corporate/files/2024-07/RenaultTrucks_GoodMove018_%20Fresques%20Climat_EN.pdf)
- Benchmark scale from another sector: Société Générale passed **40,000 employees** trained with the Climate Fresk — [Société Générale](https://www.societegenerale.com/en/news/all-news/objective-surpassed-over-40000-employees-trained-climate-fresk)
- No Renault Group-wide Climate Fresk or Renault University figure was found.

**Awards evidence**
- No 2025 IABC Gold Quill for automotive sustainability employee engagement was found. MAHLE (automotive supplier) won Gold at the German Brand Award 2025 in "Branded CSR Activities – Environmental" — [German Brand Award](https://www.german-brand-award.com/en/news/detail/mahle-wins-gold-for-sustainability-as-a-credible-strategy)

### Inferences
- Stellantis already has the three ingredients separately: Motor Citizens (action), Shares to Win (stake), and ~250k employees across plants (relay potential). The distinctive move is to **connect them into one OPOVOS-like loop**: "Own it (Shares to Win) – Voice it (annual sustainability consultation linked to FaSTLAne 2030) – Act on it (Motor Citizens + site sustainability ambassadors)".
- Schneider's Act for Green **site checklist and score** is a ready model for plant-level storytelling. Each Stellantis plant gets a visible scorecard (energy, waste, water, SUSTAINera reuse) on shopfloor screens, maintained by local ambassadors. This turns abstract group targets into plant-level, comparable facts.
- LVMH's ratio (~200 correspondents for 75 Maisons) suggests a scaled design for Stellantis: 1–2 correspondents per brand plus 1 per plant, i.e. roughly 150–200 people. This is an inference to validate.
- Opening the academy outward (Schneider Sustainability School, LVMH to partners) suits Stellantis' dealer and supplier network as a stakeholder-adapted extension.

- Siemens, SAP, Salesforce Trailhead, Michelin, Enel, Volvo Cars and Toyota employee programmes were not verified in this pass.
- No verified data was found on gamified ESG apps, shopfloor CO2 dashboards (as communication), or employee-advocacy LinkedIn programmes with sustainability content.

## Q4. Market localization and the greenhushing vs EU regulation climate (2025–2026)

### Takeaway
The same group story is told through different "proof languages": **ethanol and Bio-Hybrid in Brazil** (Stellantis itself), **jobs, plants and "multi-pathway" in the US** (Toyota), and **verifiable, specific data in the EU**. That is now legally required since EmpCo became applicable on **27 Sept 2026** (generic claims and offset-based neutrality claims banned, labels must be certified). US companies keep investing while talking less (EcoVadis 2025). The separate Green Claims Directive is still stalled.

### Cited Findings
**EU: EmpCo (Directive (EU) 2024/825), applicable since 27 Sept 2026**
- It bans generic environmental claims ("eco-friendly", "green", "sustainable") without recognised excellent environmental performance (e.g. EU Ecolabel); self-developed standards don't qualify. It bans product-level neutrality/reduction claims based on **offsetting**. Sustainability labels must be based on certification or set by public authorities; unverified labels must go. It bans whole-product/business claims that apply only to a part. There is no general transition period for old stock — [Climate Focus](https://climatefocus.com/publications/beyond-carbon-neutral-new-eu-rules-for-green-claims-apply-from-september-2026/); [McCann FitzGerald](https://www.mccannfitzgerald.com/knowledge/disputes/the-european-union-empowering-consumers-for-the-green-transition-regulations-2026-key-implications-for-businesses); [osapiens](https://osapiens.com/blog/empco-fact-check/); [Sustainability Online](https://sustainabilityonline.net/explainer/carbon-neutral-banned-in-the-eu-what-businesses-need-to-know/)
- Sources differ on offset-related claims: some say future-performance claims are allowed with a public, verifiable implementation plan; others are stricter. Legal check required — [Climate Focus](https://climatefocus.com/publications/beyond-carbon-neutral-new-eu-rules-for-green-claims-apply-from-september-2026/) vs [Sustainability Online](https://sustainabilityonline.net/explainer/carbon-neutral-banned-in-the-eu-what-businesses-need-to-know/)
- The separate Green Claims Directive proposal is still stalled in negotiations — [ShippyPro](https://www.shippypro.com/blog/en/the-green-claims-directive-greenwashing-obligations-from-september-27-2026)

**US: greenhushing**
- EcoVadis 2025 US Outlook surveyed 400 executives at companies with over $1B revenue. Most are maintaining or increasing sustainability investment but **communicating less** amid ESG backlash — [ESG Today](https://www.esgtoday.com/most-u-s-companies-growing-or-maintaining-esg-investments-but-talking-about-it-less-survey/); [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/us-companies-quietly-maintaining-boosting-123141122.html)
- South Pole: about 70% of sustainability-minded companies are deliberately hiding climate goals; 88% in environmental services report reduced messaging (date unclear) — [Impakter](https://impakter.com/companies-are-hiding-their-climate-progress-a-new-report-explains-why)
- UK 2026: 9 in 10 leaders fear their claims will be challenged (Marketing Pod "Big Green Fears"). Earthwatch/YouGov: 23.6% of medium and large firms said they had greenhushed (c. 2023) — [Earthwatch](https://earthwatch.org.uk/new-earthwatch-study-finds-1-in-4-companies-hiding-their-green-credentials/)
- **Toyota US** frames electrification as jobs plus local manufacturing: $912M and 252 jobs across 5 US plants for hybrids (Nov 2025), within up to $10B of US investment over 5 years. "Multi-pathway" is the powertrain narrative, with electrified vehicles at nearly half of US sales.
