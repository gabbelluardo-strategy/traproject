# Cross-industry (non-automotive) sustainability communication at customer touchpoints — distinctive executions

Scope note: selective set of executions where sustainability is made tangible at a concrete touchpoint (in-product/app, store/service, packaging/label, OOH/guerrilla, gaming, film). Corporate-narrative cases from the previous round (Schneider, Ørsted, Tony's, Patagonia ownership, Kering EP&L, Allbirds label) are not repeated. Research method: WebSearch only. Direct fetch of company/agency domains was blocked (WebFetch to publicis.com failed with DNS error), so figures are taken from search-result summaries of the cited pages. Treat agency/award-entry numbers as self-reported. Status checked as of Oct 2026 where possible; most sources are 2021–2025.

Shortlist of the strongest cases ("one excellent case beats two mediocre"):
1. Google Maps eco-friendly routing: an in-product default nudge (closest analogue for in-car/app eco nudges)
2. Back Market "Hack Market": AirDrop guerrilla at the point of purchase of a competitor (Cannes Silver PR, One Show Green Pencil, D&AD Yellow Pencils)
3. IKEA "Buy with your time" (Dubai) + IKEA Buy Back/Circular Hub: price tag/currency hacks and circular retail formats
4. Hellmann's food-waste ecosystem: Animal Crossing island (gaming) → Meal Reveal AI app (in-product utility)
5. Arc'teryx ReBird Service Centers / Decathlon second life + rental + repair: the circular service as a physical store format
6. Patagonia Worn Wear tour: a mobile repair truck as travelling brand media
7. Cautionary cases: Apple "carbon neutral" Watch (German court, label withdrawn), Booking.com Travel Sustainable (removed after Dutch ACM action), Oatly CO2 claims (UK ASA), H&M (Norwegian Consumer Authority / Higg; Looop as theatre rather than scale)

## Q1. What exactly is the execution, and how does it make sustainability tangible, simple and visual at the touchpoint?

### Takeaway
The distinctive executions don't explain sustainability. They put it into a decision the customer is already making (route choice, which phone to buy, how to pay, what to cook) and show it as a default, a price, a number or a service, usually at the moment of choice.

### Cited Findings
**Google Maps eco-friendly routing (in-product nudge; digital)**
- Launched Oct 2021. Maps shows the most fuel-efficient route when ETA is similar, comparing it with the fastest route. Google measured savings by comparing the fuel Maps users likely consumed on the routes they drove with the fastest route — [The Drive](https://www.thedrive.com/news/google-maps-eco-route-feature-helped-drivers-drastically-cut-tailpipe-emissions)
- Later extended to let users pick engine type (petrol, diesel, hybrid, EV) so the estimate fits the vehicle, and offered to fleets via the Routes API — [Food Logistics](https://www.foodlogistics.com/transportation/fleet-management/news/22484599/google-google-maps-ecofriendly-routing-optimizes-fleet-management); [WhichCar](https://www.whichcar.com.au/car-news/google-maps-fuel-efficient-suggestions); [Google Canada blog](https://blog.google/intl/en-ca/products/devices-services/looking-greener-trips-google-maps-has-got-you-covered-eco-friendly-routing/)

**Back Market "Hack Market" (guerrilla at the competitor's point of sale; physical + digital)**
- On Earth Day (22 Apr 2022), Back Market and agency Marcel (Publicis) used a bot to AirDrop short video ads to iPhones, iPads and MacBooks on display in Apple Stores. The ads reached shoppers while they were holding the new device and pointed them to the refurbished option. One message read: "The seller won't tell you that you can buy the same model — but reduce 92% of its carbon footprint." — [Contagious](https://contagious.com/news-and-views/campaign-of-the-week-second-hand-phone-seller-back-market-targets-apple-stores-with-airdrop-ads); [The Stable](https://www.thestable.com.au/marcel-back-market-hacks-apple-to-sell-its-refurbished-phones/)
- Claim based on an ADEME (French environment agency) study: a refurbished smartphone has about 91% less CO2 impact than a new one. Note the 91% vs 92% inconsistency between the source and the creative — [The Stable](https://www.thestable.com.au/marcel-back-market-hacks-apple-to-sell-its-refurbished-phones/)
- Ran in 6 stores in Paris, Berlin and London — [Contagious](https://contagious.com/news-and-views/campaign-of-the-week-second-hand-phone-seller-back-market-targets-apple-stores-with-airdrop-ads)

**IKEA "Buy with your time" (price tag as currency; physical retail, Dubai)**
- Memac Ogilvy Dubai. Customers used Google Maps Timeline to show the time they had spent travelling to IKEA, and that time became credit for products. One hour was valued at AED 105 (based on Dubai's average wage), and "time prices" were added to every product in store. Launched with the opening of the Jebel Ali store — [shots](https://shots.net/news/view/spend-a-little-time-with-ikea); [WARC AME](https://www.warc.com/content/article/ame/ikea-buy-with-your-time/en-gb/139729); [Brands Vietnam case film page](https://www.brandsvietnam.com/work/case-film-ikea-buy-with-your-time/)
- Note: this is mainly an affordability and time-value message, not strictly a sustainability one. Its relevance here is the mechanic: re-denominating the price tag and using location data as currency.

**IKEA Buy Back & Resell / Circular Hub / second-hand pilot (store format)**
- Old IKEA furniture is returned for store vouchers worth 50% of the original price (as new), 40% (minor scratches) or 30% (well used). Items are resold in the in-store "Circular Hub" (formerly "As-is"), recycled or donated — [BusinessGreen](https://businessgreen.com/news/4030763/circular-economy-ikea-kicks-furniture-business-nationwide); [Green Queen](https://www.greenqueen.com.hk/fully-circular-by-2030-ikea-launches-resale-hub-concept-for-secondhand-furniture/)
- First second-hand-only IKEA pilot store (2020) is in Eskilstuna, inside the ReTuna recycling mall (not in Stockholm itself). Goods come from municipal recycling centres and are repaired next door — [WEF](https://weforum.org/stories/2020/10/ikea-opens-pilot-second-hand-store-sweden-circular-economy); [FashionNetwork](https://uk.fashionnetwork.com/news/Ikea-opens-pilot-second-hand-store-in-sweden,1255787.html)

**Hellmann's food-waste platform (gaming → AI utility; digital)**
- "Hellmann's vs. Virtual Food Waste" / Hellmann's Island (Ogilvy Toronto, 2020–21). In Animal Crossing, players brought spoiled virtual turnips to Hellmann's island, and for each one Hellmann's donated a real meal to a food bank. It reused an existing in-game behaviour (turnips rot after a week) — [Cannes Lions / Love the Work](https://www.lovethework.com/work-awards/campaigns/hellmanns-vs-virtual-food-waste-708719); [Strategy Atomic Awards 2021](https://atomicawards.strategyonline.ca/winners/winner/2021/?e=110200); [Ogilvy](https://www.ogilvy.com/uk/work/hellmanns-island)
- "Meal Reveal" (Mar 2024, Food Waste Action Week; Ogilvy London). Users photograph their fridge, Google Cloud Vertex AI identifies the ingredients and suggests recipes. It tackles "fridge blindness" — [Marketing Beat](https://www.marketing-beat.co.uk/2024/03/22/hellmanns-ai-food-waste-tool/); [Google Cloud press](https://www.googlecloudpresscorner.com/2024-03-18-Hellmanns-launches-AI-enabled-tool-to-reveal-your-fridges-meal-potential); [Cannes Lions entry](https://lovethework.com/work-awards/campaigns/meal-reveal-1625051)
- Umbrella: "Make Taste, Not Waste" platform — [Cannes Lions entry](https://lovethework.com/work-awards/campaigns/hellmanns-make-taste-not-waste-1545931); [WARC](https://www.warc.com/content/article/cannes/hellmanns-make-taste-not-waste/en-gb/160307)

**Arc'teryx ReBird Service Centers (circular service as store architecture)**
- In-store service bars offering product assessment, GORE-TEX leak testing, technical washing, DWR (water-repellent) renewal, zipper/buckle/patch repairs and hands-on care education. Also sells ReBird upcycled products and refurbished "Used Gear". First centre opened Nov 2021 at 547 Broadway, NYC — [SGB](https://sgbonline.com/arcteryx-opens-first-store-with-rebird-service-center-in-nyc/); [Ecotextile](https://www.ecotextile.com/2021110843206/news/fashion-retail/arc-teryx-introduces-in-store-circular-services/)
- Boulder (Sept 2022) is the largest, with an on-site GORE wash and re-waterproofing station. Other centres in Toronto, Tokyo, Beijing and Vancouver (Kitsilano) — [GearJunkie](https://gearjunkie.com/news/arcteryx-rebird-service-center-boulder); [Arc'teryx blog](https://blog.arcteryx.com/news/the-bird-reborn-arcteryx-returns-to-its-roots-with-new-pinnacle-hometown-store-and-rebird-service-center-in-kitsilano/)

**Patagonia Worn Wear tour (mobile repair truck; physical/event)**
- A repair truck tours with staff from the Reno repair facility, fixing zippers, rips and tears for free and teaching customers to do repairs themselves — [BK Accelerator](https://bkaccelerator.com/patagonia-launches-a-worn-wear-tour-of-the-u-s/)
- Formats are tailored to each community: a "Fish Tour" (2019) and surf tours — [Patagonia Works press](https://www.patagoniaworks.com/press/2019/3/28/patagonia-announces-its-first-worn-wear-fish-tour); [Zenbird](https://zenbird.media/patagonias-worn-wear-tour-returns-and-hits-the-surf-areas/)
- The 2025 Snow Tour visited 9 winter destinations in the UK and the Alps and repaired clothing from any brand — [Horizont](https://www.horizont.net/schweiz/nachrichten/repair-event-worn-wear-snow-tour-2025-macht-auch-in-der-schweiz-halt-225609)

**Decathlon second life / rental / repair (omnichannel circular services)**
- 2024: 1.35M second-hand products sold and 3M products repaired across 1,730 repair workshops. Decathlon says it holds 50% of the rental market in Italy and Spain — [Decathlon 2024 performance](https://www.decathlon-united.media/pressfiles/decathlon-group-2024-performance)

**Klarna CO2 insights (payment app; digital)**
- Apr 2021: a carbon-footprint estimate for every purchase in the Klarna app, using Doconomy's Åland Index, free for up to 90M consumers. At launch the estimate was shown only after purchase — [Inside Retail](https://insideretail.com.au/business/swedens-klarna-offers-carbon-footprint-tracker-for-shoppers-202104); [The Industry](https://www.theindustry.fashion/klarna-launches-carbon-footprint-insights-for-90-million-consumers/)

**Apple Daisy + Trade In (reverse logistics as a story)**
- Daisy (2018) disassembles iPhones to recover materials. A German report says up to 200 iPhones per hour. It launched together with an Earth Day trade-in donation to Conservation International — [Apple Newsroom 2018](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2018/04/apple-adds-earth-day-donations-to-trade-in-and-recycling-program/); [WinFuture video](https://winfuture.de/videos/Hardware/Wie-Apple-alte-iPhones-zerlegt-Neuer-Recycling-Roboter-Daisy-19073.html); [Apple Newsroom 2019](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2019/04/apple-expands-global-recycling-programs/)
- "Mother Nature" (Sept 2023, Wonderlust event): a 5-minute film in which Octavia Spencer as Mother Nature questions Tim Cook and his executives about the 2030 carbon-neutral goal. Directed by Rhys Thomas (Stink) — [shots](https://shots.net/news/view/apple-ceo-tim-cook-takes-on-mother-nature); [Ad Age](https://adage.com/article/special-report-agency-list-creativity-awards/best-production-companies-2024-stink/2543556)

**H&M Looop (in-store spectacle)**
- Launched Stockholm 2020. A machine visible in the store shreds old garments and spins and knits them into a new item while the customer watches. H&M says no water or chemicals are used, though some virgin material is added. Price SEK 100 for members, SEK 150 for non-members — [FashionUnited](https://fashionunited.uk/news/retail/h-m-launches-the-first-and-only-in-store-recycling-machine/2020100851304); [Hypebae](https://hypebae.com/2020/10/hm-looop-sustainability-in-store-material-recycling-machine-stockholm)

**Oatly on-pack climate footprint**
- Products carry a CO2e per kg figure, calculated grower to grocer with CarbonCloud. For example, Barista Edition is 0.44 kg CO2e/kg vs 1.6 for whole cow's milk. A US climate label was also launched — [FoodNavigator](https://www.foodnavigator.com/Article/2022/01/27/oatly-misleads-consumers-with-environmental-claims-rules-asa/); [Just Drinks](https://www.just-drinks.com/news/oatly-launches-climate-label-in-us)

### Inferences
- Four mechanics recur: (a) **the default/choice architecture** (Google Maps); (b) **the comparison at the moment of purchase** (Back Market's "same model, -92% CO2"); (c) **a service visible in the store** (ReBird, Looop, Worn Wear truck, Daisy) that turns an abstract commitment into something staff do in front of you; (d) **putting sustainability into a game or utility** (Hellmann's) rather than an ad.
- The more distinctive cases rarely use the word "sustainable" (Maps says "most fuel-efficient", Back Market says "refurbished, -92% footprint"). This is also safer under ECGT (see Q4).

### Gaps
- No direct visual URLs (image/video files) could be verified. Case-film pages are listed instead (Love the Work, Brands Vietnam, WinFuture, Ogilvy). The Cannes case-film page for Hack Market is https://www.lovethework.com/work-awards/campaigns/hack-market-774685 (not fetched).
- Could not verify whether Daisy is physically shown in Apple Stores. Sources only cover press and video.
- Current status of Klarna CO2 insights (2026) not found. It may have been quietly dropped; flag as unverified.

## Q2. How is each execution tied to the business model (revenue, new services, retention)?

### Takeaway
The strongest cases are commercial. The sustainable choice is the product (refurbished phones, second-hand furniture, repair or rental), so the communication drives revenue, store traffic or retention. Pure awareness pieces (the Apple film, Looop) are weaker and draw greenwashing fire.

### Cited Findings
- **Back Market**: refurbished electronics is the whole business model, so the CO2 claim is the sales argument, aimed at the exact moment of purchase of a new device — [Contagious](https://contagious.com/news-and-views/campaign-of-the-week-second-hand-phone-seller-back-market-targets-apple-stores-with-airdrop-ads)
- **IKEA Buy Back** pays in store vouchers, not cash, so a circular return becomes a store visit and future spend. Resale also runs online (e.g., the UK Gumtree partnership) — [BusinessGreen](https://businessgreen.com/news/4030763/circular-economy-ikea-kicks-furniture-business-nationwide)
- **IKEA Buy with your time** was tied to a new store opening and measured on footfall (+30% vs KPI per WARC; 32% per Brands Vietnam) — [WARC](https://www.warc.com/content/article/ame/ikea-buy-with-your-time/en-gb/139729); [Brands Vietnam](https://www.brandsvietnam.com/work/case-film-ikea-buy-with-your-time/)
- **Decathlon**: revenue from circular models grew 10.4% in 2024 (per its non-financial statement as cited by Thiga). Circular share of turnover is reported as 2.64% in 2025 (executive's LinkedIn, +24%) vs "3.5% today" (Thiga, Oct 2025). Definitions likely differ. Target is 25% by 2035 — [Thiga](https://www.media.thiga.co/en/decathlon-bets-on-digital-to-accelerate-circularity); [LinkedIn – L. Fontinoy](https://es.linkedin.com/in/laurencefontinoy); [IMD case](https://www.imd.org/research-knowledge/sustainability/case-studies/decathlon-s-circular-revolution-scaling-sustainable-business-models)
- Decathlon India aims to triple circular revenue by 2027 vs 2024 — [Indian Retailer](https://indianretailer.com/news/decathlon-expands-repair-resale-and-buyback-services-across-india-scale-circular-retail)
- A Decathlon-linked article says circularity improves customer loyalty and drives profitable sales — [IPC](https://ipc.be/news-portal/e-commerce/2024/06/10/09/12/circularity-improves-customer-loyalty-drives-profitable-sales-growth)
- **Arc'teryx ReBird**: an after-sales service (paid washes and repairs), a resale line and upcycled product, all inside flagship stores. Revenue figures not public — [SGB](https://sgbonline.com/arcteryx-opens-first-store-with-rebird-service-center-in-nyc/)
- **Apple Trade In** pays out in purchase credit or Apple Store gift cards, which feeds the upgrade cycle — [Apple Newsroom 2018](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2018/04/apple-adds-earth-day-donations-to-trade-in-and-recycling-program/)
- **H&M Looop** is a paid service (SEK 100–150) with a member discount, tying it to the loyalty program. H&M's head of sustainability said it is not meant to recycle at industrial scale — [Hypebae](https://hypebae.com/2020/10/hm-looop-sustainability-in-store-material-recycling-machine-stockholm); [WarpNews](https://www.warpnews.org/green-tech/looop-an-in-store-recycling-machine/)
- **Hellmann's** positions the brand as useful with leftovers (mayo as the "ingredient that saves leftovers"), linked to category usage — [Unilever](https://www.unilever.com/news/news-search/2022/behind-the-brand-whats-hellmanns-secret-sauce-for-success/)
- **Google Maps** eco routing extended to a B2B product (Routes API for fleets) — [SDCExec](https://www.sdcexec.com/transportation/fleet-management/news/22484599/google-google-maps-ecofriendly-routing-optimizes-fleet-management)

### Inferences
- For automotive the closest models are Decathlon (repair + rental + second life across a big network) and IKEA (vouchers that turn returns into new purchases). These map to certified used/refurbished vehicles, battery repair and second life, subscription/rental, and parts remanufacturing. Stellantis already runs a circular business unit (SUSTAINera, not researched here) that could become a customer-facing touchpoint brand.
- Loyalty-linked circular services (H&M member pricing, IKEA vouchers) are a simple way to tie behaviour to retention.

### Gaps
- No revenue numbers found for ReBird, Worn Wear trucks, Nike Refurbished or the IKEA Circular Hub (2025–26).
- Nike Refurbished: only 2021 launch coverage found (15 US stores, mostly outlets). Current scale and status not verified — [Retail Dive](https://www.retaildive.com/news/nike-launches-refurbishment-program-in-15-stores/598216/)

## Q3. How is it adapted across markets and stakeholders?

### Takeaway
The best brands keep one global idea and change the local "currency": local wage (IKEA Dubai), the national environment agency's data (ADEME for Back Market), local communities (Worn Wear Fish/Surf/Snow tours), local rental share (Decathlon in Italy and Spain), and regulator-driven label changes by market (Booking, Apple, Oatly).

### Cited Findings
- IKEA Dubai priced time at the local average wage (AED 105/hour) — [shots](https://shots.net/news/view/spend-a-little-time-with-ikea)
- Back Market grounded its claim in a French public-agency study (ADEME) and ran across FR/DE/UK stores — [The Stable](https://www.thestable.com.au/marcel-back-market-hacks-apple-to-sell-its-refurbished-phones/)
- Worn Wear tours are tailored by community (fishing, surf, snow) and by region (US, Europe, UK/Alps). A past European tour visited 7 countries and repaired over 2,000 garments — [Patagonia Works](https://www.patagoniaworks.com/press/2019/3/28/patagonia-announces-its-first-worn-wear-fish-tour); [L'Adige](https://www.ladige.it/salute-e-benessere/worn-wear-con-patagonia-a-trento-per-riparare-gratis-i-vostri-capi-rotti-xwinjjhg); [Horizont](https://www.horizont.net/schweiz/nachrichten/repair-event-worn-wear-snow-tour-2025-macht-auch-in-der-schweiz-halt-225609)
- Google adapted eco routing to engine type and regions (e.g., Canada blog) and to fleets (B2B stakeholder) — [Google Canada blog](https://blog.google/intl/en-ca/products/devices-services/looking-greener-trips-google-maps-has-got-you-covered-eco-friendly-routing/); [Food Logistics](https://www.foodlogistics.com/transportation/fleet-management/news/22484599/google-google-maps-ecofriendly-routing-optimizes-fleet-management)
- Hellmann's rolled out platform executions by market: Canada (Animal Crossing island), UK (Meal Reveal tied to WRAP's Food Waste Action Week) — [Atomic Awards](https://atomicawards.strategyonline.ca/winners/winner/2021/?e=110200); [Marketing Beat](https://www.marketing-beat.co.uk/2024/03/22/hellmanns-ai-food-waste-tool/)
- Decathlon localises circular services (India buyback/repair rollout; rental leadership in IT/ES) — [Indian Retailer](https://indianretailer.com/news/decathlon-expands-repair-resale-and-buyback-services-across-india-scale-circular-retail); [Decathlon 2024](https://www.decathlon-united.media/pressfiles/decathlon-group-2024-performance)
- Arc'teryx put ReBird Service Centers in flagship cities across North America and Asia (NYC, Boulder, Toronto, Vancouver, Tokyo, Beijing) — [Arc'teryx blog](https://blog.arcteryx.com/news/the-bird-reborn-arcteryx-returns-to-its-roots-with-new-pinnacle-hometown-store-and-rebird-service-center-in-kitsilano/)

### Inferences
- For a 14-brand portfolio: one group-level mechanic (e.g., an eco-routing/eco-driving nudge or a refurbished/second-life label) can be "skinned" per brand and per market (local energy mix, local incentives, local trusted data source such as ADEME/ISPRA/UBA), the way Hellmann's runs one platform across markets.
- Employee/HR angle: the repair technicians in Worn Wear and ReBird are the face of the message. Dealer technicians could play the same role (inference, not sourced).

### Gaps
- No sources on how these brands brief internal stakeholders and employees on these touchpoints.

## Q4. Effectiveness evidence and greenwashing/regulatory risk

### Takeaway
The award winners are behaviour- and product-led (Back Market, Hellmann's, IKEA). The regulatory casualties are badges and offset-based neutrality claims (Booking, Apple, Oatly's comparative claims, H&M/Higg). From 27 Sept 2026 the EU Empowering Consumers Directive (ECGT) bans generic claims and offset-based product neutrality claims. The proposed Green Claims Directive was withdrawn in June 2025.

### Cited Findings
**Effectiveness**
- Google Maps eco routing avoided about 1.2M tonnes of CO2 from Oct 2021 to Dec 2022 (similar to 250k ICE cars a year), per Google's environmental report. An earlier update said over 0.5Mt. Google projects more than 1Mt a year globally. The method compares against the fastest route — [The Drive](https://www.thedrive.com/news/google-maps-eco-route-feature-helped-drivers-drastically-cut-tailpipe-emissions); [Google Canada blog](https://blog.google/intl/en-ca/products/devices-services/looking-greener-trips-google-maps-has-got-you-covered-eco-friendly-routing/)
- Back Market Hack Market reached an estimated 5,200 shoppers (accepted AirDrops) and 100M+ social impressions in the following week (agency figures). Awards: Cannes Lions Silver PR, 2023 One Show Green Pencil, and D&AD (3 Yellow, 2 Graphite, 5 Wood Pencils). The Drum called it the most-awarded retail campaign of 2023 — [Contagious](https://contagious.com/news-and-views/campaign-of-the-week-second-hand-phone-seller-back-market-targets-apple-stores-with-airdrop-ads); [D&AD](https://www.dandad.org/work/d-ad-awards-archive/hack-market); [The One Show](https://www.oneclub.org/awards/theoneshow/-award/48469/hack-market); [Cannes Lions](https://www.lovethework.com/work-awards/campaigns/hack-market-774685); [The Drum](https://www.thedrum.com/news/the-world-s-most-awarded-retail-campaigns)
- Back Market also has a WARC effectiveness entry, "New is Old" — [WARC](https://warc.com/content/article/warc-awards-effectiveness/back-market-new-is-old/150757)
- Hellmann's Island: 66.1M impressions, 54.8M reach, 6.8M engagements (46.7% engagement rate), 5,000+ tour requests on Twitter, budget about $600k (award entry, self-reported) — [Atomic Awards](https://atomicawards.strategyonline.ca/winners/winner/2021/?e=110200)
- Meal Reveal reached 16M UK households and 200M global media impressions (vendor case study). Cannes 2024 entry, medal not verified — [Publicis Sapient](https://www.publicissapient.com/work/hellmanns-unilever-ai-food-waste-app); [Cannes Lions entry](https://lovethework.com/work-awards/campaigns/meal-reveal-1625051)
- IKEA Buy with your time: footfall +30–32%. Cannes outcome conflicts across sources (Titanium shortlist 2020 per Love the Work; Gold/Bronze per a creative's portfolio, unverified). AME Silver per WARC — [Love the Work](https://lovethework.com/work-awards/entries/561679); [WARC](https://www.warc.com/content/article/ame/ikea-buy-with-your-time/en-gb/139729)

**Greenwashing / regulation**
- **Apple**: a Frankfurt court (2025, case brought by Deutsche Umwelthilfe) ruled the Apple Watch "CO2-neutral" claim misleading. The offsets came from a Paraguay eucalyptus project whose leases on 75% of the land were only secure until 2029, while consumers would expect offsets to last to about 2050. Apple said it would drop the "carbon neutral" label for Apple Watch in line with the EU rules applying from Sept 2026. A US class action targets its offsets in China and Kenya — [Trellis](https://trellis.net/article/apple-carbon-neutral-german-court-case/); [Seneca ESG](https://senecaesg.com/insights/german-court-strikes-down-apples-carbon-neutral-watch-claim); [ESG Post](https://esgpost.com/german-court-blocks-apples-co₂-neutral-watch-claim/). DISCONTINUED: Apple's carbon-neutral product label.
- **Booking.com**: Travel Sustainable (a 1–3+ level badge with green leaf icons) was taken offline from 25 March 2024 after action by the Dutch ACM, which said the name could suggest travel itself is sustainable and that it was unclear what the scores measured. Booking moved to labels and filters based only on third-party certification — [ACM](https://acm.nl/en/node/25164); [PhocusWire](https://www.phocuswire.com/booking-removes-travel-sustainable-badge); [Skift](https://skift.com/2024/03/25/booking-com-ends-possibly-misleading-sustainability-program/amp/). DISCONTINUED.
- **Oatly**: UK ASA (Jan 2022, 109 complaints) found "73% less CO2e vs milk" misleading because it was read as covering all Oatly products vs all milk. A claim that dairy/meat emit more than all transport was also misleading because the lifecycle stages compared were not equivalent. Oatly said it should have specified "vs whole milk, grower to grocer" — [FoodNavigator](https://www.foodnavigator.com/Article/2022/01/27/oatly-misleads-consumers-with-environmental-claims-rules-asa/)
- **H&M**: the Norwegian Consumer Authority warned H&M, Norrøna and the Sustainable Apparel Coalition that Higg-index-based marketing claims could be misleading/illegal. A US lawsuit also challenges H&M's sustainability advertising — [Dress-ecode](https://dress-ecode.com/en/tag/hm-en/)
- **EU ECGT (Directive 2024/825)** applies from 27 Sept 2026. It bans generic environmental claims ("eco-friendly", "green", "sustainable") without recognised excellent environmental performance (e.g., EU Ecolabel or an ISO 14024 Type I label), bans product-level climate-neutrality claims based on offsets, and restricts self-made sustainability labels. Sources say there is no transition period for live claims, many member states missed the transposition deadline (infringement procedures against 20 states, May 2026), and fines can reach 4% of turnover (vendor sources; check national law) — [CMS](https://cms.law/en/deu/legal-updates/empowering-consumers-directive-a-turning-point-for-environmental-advertising); [verso](https://verso.de/en/blog/from-green-claims-direcitve-to-empco/); [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/consumer-law/1821346/green-claims-under-the-microscope-new-rules-for-eu-and-non-eu-businesses); [C2C guidance PDF](https://api.c2ccertified.org/assets/pol_green-claims-guidance_final_040926.pdf)
- Green Claims Directive withdrawn June 2025, never adopted — [verso](https://verso.de/en/blog/from-green-claims-direcitve-to-empco/)

### Inferences
- What survives regulation is specific, comparative, scoped, third-party-backed claims (Back Market + ADEME; Oatly after it specified "vs whole milk, grower to grocer"). Also safe: utilities that report measured behaviour (Maps' tonnes avoided) rather than labelling the product "green".
- Self-made badges (Booking's leaves) and offset-based "neutral" labels (Apple) are the exact formats the ECGT outlaws. That matters for any brand-level "eco" badges on car configurators or dealer displays.
- Hack Market-style guerrilla carries legal/IP risk (it targeted another company's stores). It works for a challenger, less so for an incumbent OEM.

### Gaps
- No UK CMA case found specific to these brands in this round (the CMA Green Claims Code applies in general; the CMA's fashion investigation of ASOS/Boohoo/Asda is outside this scope).
- Did not verify the Apple appeal status or the outcome of the US offset lawsuit (as of Oct 2026).

## Q5. What could an automotive portfolio like Stellantis concretely transfer?

### Takeaway
Transferable ideas are about mechanics, not messages: in-car and app eco defaults (Maps), comparison at the point of sale (Back Market), a currency or price tag that makes footprint or longevity visible (IKEA), circular service bars in showrooms (ReBird/Decathlon), mobile repair/education tours (Worn Wear), and gamified or AI utilities (Hellmann's). Each needs claims that are compliant with the ECGT.

### Cited Findings (basis for transfer)
- Eco routing worked as an opt-out default with measured impact (1.2Mt in about 14 months) — [The Drive](https://www.thedrive.com/news/google-maps-eco-route-feature-helped-drivers-drastically-cut-tailpipe-emissions)
- Engine-type-aware routing (including EV) is already offered, so OEM navigation and apps compete with it — [WhichCar](https://www.whichcar.com.au/car-news/google-maps-fuel-efficient-suggestions)
- A comparative, agency-backed footprint claim delivered at the moment of choice won major creative and PR awards — [D&AD](https://www.dandad.org/work/d-ad-awards-archive/hack-market)
- Circular services at scale (1,730 workshops, 3M repairs, 1.35M second-hand items) are communicated as volume proof points — [Decathlon 2024](https://www.decathlon-united.media/pressfiles/decathlon-group-2024-performance)

### Inferences (proposals for the report, not sourced facts)
1. **"Eco-route" in navigation and the app as a default, with a monthly "kg CO2 / € saved" summary** in the brand apps (Jeep, Fiat, Peugeot, etc.), modelled on Maps. Report real measured savings in the ESG report, as Google did. This is a behavioural claim, not a product "green" claim, so it is lower risk under ECGT.
2. **A "same car, X% less footprint" comparison at the dealer and in the configurator** for certified used, refurbished or remanufactured parts and second-life batteries, backed by a third-party LCA (Back Market + ADEME model). Avoid "green/eco/sustainable" wording without a certified label.
3. **Showroom service bar (ReBird model)**: battery health check, eco-driving coaching and parts remanufacture shown live. Technicians become the communicators, which links to the HR/employer-brand story.
4. **Mobile tour (Worn Wear model)**: a branded van doing free battery-health checks and EV-charging education, tailored by community (rural Italy, Brazil, US Jeep owners).
5. **Price-tag re-denomination (IKEA Dubai model)**: show cost per km, or fuel/CO2 avoided over 5 years, on the price card. Only with a scoped, verifiable methodology (lesson from Oatly).
6. **Gaming/AI utility (Hellmann's model)**: e.g., an in-game EV-range or eco-driving challenge on platforms where the audience already plays (Fiat/Abarth with younger targets), linked to a real-world donation or charging credit.
7. **What to avoid**: offset-based "carbon neutral" vehicle or brand labels (Apple), self-made eco badges and leaf icons on configurators (Booking), unscoped "X% less CO2" headline comparisons (Oatly), and in-store spectacle without scale (Looop critique).

### Gaps
- No automotive-specific evidence was gathered on whether dealer-level circular messaging moves sales. That would need Stellantis or peer data (e.g., other OEMs' refurbished/certified used programmes), which is outside this round's non-automotive scope.
- Not covered due to tool-call budget (candidates for a follow-up): Lavazza 2030 calendar, Carlsberg Green Fibre Bottle, Prada Re-Nylon education programme, Mastercard Priceless Planet, Lush Naked shops, Too Good To Go, Levi's/Vinted campaigns, IKEA ThisAbles, Patagonia Footprint Chronicles (status 2026 unverified), Nike Move to Zero (status unverified).
