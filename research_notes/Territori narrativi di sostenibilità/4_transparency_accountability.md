## Q1. What do leading brands disclose beyond the mandatory report, in what format, how often and how visually?

### Takeaway
"Beyond CSRD" transparency comes in six recurring forms:
1. **Higher-frequency scoring:** Schneider's quarterly impact score, published with financial results.
2. **Standalone, versioned transition plans:** Mercedes CTAP, updated every year. Unilever's CTAP goes to a shareholder vote.
3. **Open methodologies:** Kering EP&L, Polestar LCA, Natura IP&L.
4. **Product-level proof:** Polestar's per-model LCA and labels.
5. **Report hubs with data layers:** Microsoft, Novo Nordisk ESG portal.
6. **Early experiments with AI chat assistants on reports:** Sumitomo, trial launched Sept 2026.

No automaker runs a real-time or quarterly ESG data hub comparable to Schneider's.

### Cited Findings
**Frequency: quarterly scorecards**
- Schneider Electric reports a composite impact score every quarter, alongside its financials.
  - SSI 2021–25 closed at 8.86/10 in Q4 2025.
  - The new "Impact 2030" score was 3.40 in Q1 2026 and 3.69 in H1 2026, against a 2026 target of 4.20 and a 2030 target of 10/10. It was published with H1 financial results.
  - Sources (prior round): [Electrical Times](https://www.electricaltimes.co.uk/?p=34589); [Automation Magazine](https://www.automationmagazine.co.uk/schneider-electric-reports-strong-first-half-year-results-under-new-sustainability-roadmap-impact-2030/); [tedmag Q3 2025](https://tedmag.com/schneider-electric-updates-q3-2025-sustainability-impact-report/)

**Standalone, versioned transition plans (automotive)**
- Mercedes-Benz publishes a Climate Transition Action Plan (CTAP) every year as a separate PDF, with 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026 editions. The 2026 edition calls itself "its detailed implementation plan for decarbonisation". Ambitions by 2039 vs 2019:
  - −90% CO₂ in own production;
  - −80% in the use phase, "as market conditions allow";
  - −70% across the value chain;
  - net zero "by 2049 at the latest".
  - Sources: [CTAP 2026 PDF](https://group.mercedes-benz.com/documents/investors/reports/annual-report/mercedes-benz/mercedes-benz-climate-transition-action-plan-2026-en.pdf); [CTAP page](https://group.mercedes-benz.com/sustainability/climate/ctap.html); [CTAP 2023 [dated]](https://group.mercedes-benz.com/documents/investors/reports/annual-report/mercedes-benz/mercedes-benz-climate-transition-action-plan-2023-en.pdf); [CTAP 2024](https://brandwall.api.mercedes-benz.com/documents/investors/reports/annual-report/mercedes-benz/mercedes-benz-climate-transition-action-plan-2024-en.pdf); [CTAP 2025](https://group.mercedes-benz.com/documents/investors/reports/annual-report/mercedes-benz/mercedes-benz-climate-transition-action-plan-2025.pdf)
- No search result showed an independent external review of the Mercedes CTAP (see Gaps). Mercedes also runs an investor "Sustainability Update" event and a "targets and metrics summary" PDF (prior round): [MB Sustainability Update 2025](https://group.mercedes-benz.com/investors/events/2025-mercedes-benz-group-sustainability-update.html); [ESG at MB](https://group.mercedes-benz.com/investors/esg/)

**Open methodologies**
- **Polestar:**
  - Publishes the lifetime carbon footprint of all its models "along with the entire methodology we used", explicitly to push other automakers to do the same. The method follows ISO LCA standards and the GHG Protocol Product Standard.
  - Newer reports exist (e.g. Polestar 3 MY26–27 Chengdu); for the full methodology they point back to an earlier report.
  - It is not released under an open-source licence.
  - Sources: [Polestar LCA page](https://www.polestar.com/de-ch/sustainability/reports/life-cycle-assessment); [Polestar 3 LCA PDF](https://www.polestar.com/dato-assets/11286/1778497894-polestar-3_lca_report_my26-my27_chengdu.pdf); [Polestar 2 LCA](https://polestar.com/ie/news/polestar-2-lca-report); [Renewable Energy Magazine 2020 [dated]](https://renewableenergymagazine.com/electric_hybrid_vehicles/polestar-to-publish-full-details-of-impact-20200916)
  - The annual report is branded editorially as "The Beep Report", and model pages carry product labels (prior round): [Polestar Beep Report](https://www.polestar.com/en-il/news/the-beep-report/)
- **Kering EP&L [dated, mostly 2015–2020]:**
  - Kering released its EP&L methodology as an open-source tool. It monetises GHG emissions, air and water pollution, water consumption, waste and land use, with a seven-step guide built with PwC.
  - It discloses results, methodology and research on an open website.
  - Target: −40% EP&L intensity by 2025 vs 2015. Reported result: −33% by 2020.
  - Sources: [Sustainable Brands](https://sustainablebrands.com/read/kering-releases-ep-l-methodology-to-encourage-wider-adoption-of-natural-capital-accounting); [WBCSD archive](https://archive.wbcsd.org/Archive/Assess-and-Manage-Performance/Measuring-and-valuing-impact-business-examples/Kering-Environmental-Profit-and-Loss-EP-L-accounting); [FCLTGlobal](https://www.fcltglobal.org/resource/kering/). Whether the 2025 target was met was not found.
- **Natura &Co IP&L:**
  - The Integrated Profit & Loss monetises environmental, social and human impacts across the full value chain, from Amazon communities to consultants and end of life. The work began in 2010 (EP&L), added social P&L in 2018, and became the combined IP&L in 2021.
  - Natura uses it for management decisions, risk and investor reporting.
  - Headline: every BRL 1 of revenue generated BRL 1.5 of positive social and environmental impact in 2021.
  - Sources: [IFAC case study](https://www.ifac.org/knowledge-gateway/discussion/case-study-integrated-profit-and-loss-accounting-natura-co); [Chapter Zero](https://hub.chapterzeroalliance.org/article/case-study-natura-co-ipl-circular-carbon-and-a-triple-bottom-line-approach); [Business Fights Poverty 2025](https://businessfightspoverty.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Natura-Case-Study.pdf)

**Report hubs and data layers**
- Microsoft's hub runs from 4 commitments, to proof numbers, to stories, to a reports hub with a GRI index and the annual report (prior round, fetched Oct 2026).
  - The 2026 Environmental Sustainability Report covers FY2025 against a 2020 baseline, with a foreword co-signed by Brad Smith and CSO Melanie Nakagawa.
  - Sources: [microsoft.com sustainability](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/corporate-responsibility/sustainability); [reports hub](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/corporate-responsibility/reports-hub); [GRI index](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/corporate-responsibility/gri-index); [ABES summary](https://abes.org.br/microsoft-construindo-o-futuro-da-ia-com-responsabilidade-por-brad-smith-e-melanie-nakagawa/)
  - No separate interactive data fact sheet was confirmed.
- Novo Nordisk runs an "ESG portal" with separate pages for the Sustainability statement (CSRD since 2024, based on a double materiality assessment) and "transparency disclosures": [Sustainability statement](https://www.novonordisk.com/sustainable-business/esg-portal/sustainability-statement.html); [Transparency disclosures](https://novonordisk.com/sustainable-business/esg-portal/transparency-disclosures-.html)
- BMW has a long-running interactive online annual report. EQS won a Grand ARC Award in 2016 for it **[dated]**, and the 2023 online report was built by 3st kommunikation: [EQS](https://eqs-news.com/news/corporate/eqs-group-wins-grand-arc-award-for-bmw-groups-interactive-annual-report/960665); [red dot interview](https://red-dot.org/magazine/interview-3st-kommunikation-gmbh)

**AI assistants on report data**
- Sumitomo Corporation (30 Sept 2026) introduced an "Integrated Report 2026 Chatbot" on a trial basis, available until 23 Dec 2026. Provided by Wills Inc. as an AI "Interactive Report", it lets stakeholders ask natural-language questions. Sumitomo also says it improved the report's "AI-readability": [Sumitomo Corp](https://www.sumitomocorp.com/en/jp/news/important/group/20260930)
- No automaker was found running a public AI assistant on its sustainability data.
  - BMW's GAIA is an internal employee platform: [MarkLines](https://www.marklines.com/en/news/336080).
  - BMW's July 2026 ChatGPT plugin is a product configurator, not ESG.
- Academic caution: generative AI in sustainability reporting could facilitate greenwashing: [Univ. Pretoria](https://www.repository.up.ac.za/handle/2263/94035?show=full)

**Assurance beyond the legal minimum**
- Novo Nordisk has a long record of voluntary assurance **[dated]**:
  - its annual report was assured both by an independent provider and against AA1000AS (2014);
  - Deloitte's 2003 assurance published its management recommendations online.
  - Sources: [UNGC CoP 2014](https://unglobalcompact.org/participation/report/cop/advanced/126821); [UNGC 2003](https://unglobalcompact.org/participation/report/cop/detail/6820)
- No current (2025–26) example was found of an automaker taking reasonable assurance, rather than the CSRD default of limited assurance, on climate KPIs (see Gaps).

### Inferences
- The strongest "beyond compliance" devices are **rhythm** (quarterly), **versioning** (a plan with a year in its title, updated every year), **openness of method** (others can replicate or challenge it) and **product-level proof**. Volume of data is not what sets leaders apart.
- AI chat on reports is emerging (Sumitomo is a trial). It is a first-mover opportunity, but it carries greenwashing and accuracy risk under EmpCo. Any assistant would need answers grounded only in the audited statement, with citations.

### Gaps
- Whether Mercedes' CTAP (or any automaker's plan) has an external independent review as EmpCo requires for future-performance claims. Nothing was found.
- Schneider weightings and Microsoft data fact-sheet availability are unverified.
- Interface, Danone, Apple, Hyundai/Kia, Toyota and BYD "beyond compliance" formats were not found within budget, beyond what the prior rounds cover.

## Q2. How do they handle bad news and target changes (first person, causes, new path)?

### Takeaway
Credible handling has four parts:
1. Name the miss in the company's own voice, with the number.
2. Give the cause.
3. Keep or re-state the long-term goal.
4. Show the new path.

Microsoft, Google, Patagonia, Ingka and Volvo do this in different ways. Unilever (2024) is the counter-example: the new targets were framed as "realistic", without a narrative of what was learned. Stellantis' 2038 → 2050 change currently has only third-party narration.

### Cited Findings
- **Microsoft:** emissions rose about 25% in 2025, to about 20 MtCO2e, attributed to data-centre construction. In the prior round, leaders were quoted as saying solutions are "not scaling fast enough" ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/07/09/microsoft-carbon-emissions-2025-data-centers/)). This round could not re-confirm the exact quote in the report foreword; the rise is also reported by [The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2026/07/10/microsofts-emissions-surged-25-in-2025-during-data-centre-boom).
- **Google:**
  - In its 2024 report it dropped its 17-year "operational carbon neutrality" claim, with emissions up about 50% since 2019: [Fortune 2024 [dated]](https://www.fortune.com/2024/07/10/ai-has-destroyed-googles-promise-of-carbon-neutrality-with-emissions-rising-50-over-the-last-five-years).
  - The latest report shows +18% year on year and +81% vs the 2019 baseline, with Scope 3 up 25%. It keeps the "intentionally ambitious" net zero 2030 goal, admits that "reaching our climate moonshots is getting harder", and counters with avoided emissions: [ESG Dive](https://esgdive.com/news/googles-emissions-continue-to-climb-due-to-ai-buildout/824373); [Trellis](https://trellis.net/article/google-isnt-abandoning-its-2030-goal-despite-another-big-emissions-increase)
  - Report editions and years are ambiguous across sources.
- **Volvo Cars (Sept 2024) [dated]:**
  - Replaced "fully electric by 2030" with 90–100% electrified (BEV plus PHEV), keeping up to 10% mild hybrids.
  - Causes named: market conditions, slow charging rollout, incentive withdrawals, tariffs.
  - CEO Jim Rowan: "We are resolute in our belief that our future is electric … the transition to electrification will not be linear, and customers and markets are moving at different speeds."
  - Net zero 2040 was kept.
  - Sources: [Electronic Specifier](https://www.electronicspecifier.com/industries/automotive/volvo-cuts-2030-ev-targets-cites-market-conditions); [ESG Today](https://www.esgtoday.com/volvo-cars-pulls-back-electrification-carbon-reduction-goals); [The Driven](https://thedriven.io/2024/09/05/volvo-cars-dumps-2030-all-electric-target-to-ramp-up-hybrids-as-it-weakens-climate-goals)
- **Polestar:** the Polestar 0 "truly climate-neutral car" moved from 2030 to 2035 in the 2025 report (prior round): [Automotive World](https://www.automotiveworld.com/news/polestar-revises-net-zero-car-deadline-to-2035/)
- **Mercedes:** the use-phase target is qualified "as market conditions allow" in CTAP 2026, with net zero by 2049: [CTAP 2026](https://group.mercedes-benz.com/documents/investors/reports/annual-report/mercedes-benz/mercedes-benz-climate-transition-action-plan-2026-en.pdf). T&E projected Mercedes as the only EU automaker missing 2025 fleet CO₂ limits on its own, needing to pool with Volvo and Polestar: [T&E](https://www.transportenvironment.org/te-deutschland/articles/analyse-mercedes-verpasst-als-einziger-eu-autohersteller-die-co2-flottengrenzwerte)
- **Patagonia** (prior round):
  - FY25 footprint up 2%; 2025 preferred-materials goal missed (about 84–86% vs 100%).
  - The chief impact officer called the report "a transparency tool, not a victory lap".
  - Sources: [retailboss](https://retailboss.co/patagonia-impact-report); [Trellis](https://trellis.net/article/patagonias-comprehensive-plan-counter-rising-emissions/)
- **Ingka/IKEA** (prior round): deferred the FY25 total footprint to FY26 because of a methodology revision, stated openly. The last full footprint was −30.1% vs FY16, against a −50% FY30 target: [ingka.com](https://www.ingka.com/?p=164513)
- **Unilever (counter-example, April 2024) [dated]** (prior round): plastics and living-wage targets were cut. The CEO called them "intentionally and unashamedly realistic", and Greenpeace condemned the change: [Food Ingredients First](https://www.foodingredientsfirst.com/news/unilever-u-turn-multinational-faces-criticism-after-esg-strategy-rethink.html)
- **Stellantis:**
  - The 2025 annual report states the update to −20/30% absolute GHG by 2030 vs 2021 and net zero 2050: [Stellantis AR 2025 (sustainabilityreports.com)](https://www.sustainabilityreports.com/stellantis/2025/annual-report-1)
  - Reported 2025 progress: −26% Scope 1-3 and −43% Scope 1-2: [Toward Carbon Net Zero](https://www.stellantis.com/en/sustainability/toward-carbon-net-zero)
  - The "why" found comes from press commentary (EV absorption slower in Europe; a shift under CEO Antonio Filosa), not a company first-person story: [ClubAlfa](https://www.clubalfa.it/en/stellantis-delays-its-green-targets-and-moves-net-zero-to-2050-35759)
  - The Climate Policy Report 2025 attributes the reset to "market dynamics and government policy" (prior round): [MarketScreener](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/stellantis-n-climate-policy-report-stellantis-2025-climate-policy-report-ce7e51ddda88ff22)

### Inferences
- Volvo's 2024 message is the closest automotive template for Stellantis. It has one sentence of belief, an explicit list of causes, a new range target, and the unchanged long-term goal. Volvo did, however, receive "weakens climate goals" headlines, so candour limits the damage but does not prevent it.
- Google and Microsoft show that a big-number miss can coexist with leadership perception if it comes with (a) the cause, (b) mitigating evidence and (c) a retained goal. Stellantis has a positive fact available (−26% Scope 1-3 already achieved in 2025) but did not use it to frame the reset.

### Gaps
- No primary Stellantis first-person statement explaining 2038 → 2050 was found.
- Exact Microsoft foreword wording is unverified this round.

## Q3. How is transparency linked to business strategy and governance (pay, board committees, capital markets)?

### Takeaway
The auto peers with the clearest accountability mechanics are BMW and VW:
- **BMW:** an EU fleet-CO₂ target carries 25% weight in its LTI scorecard, and about 37% of variable target pay is ESG-linked.
- **VW:** the Decarbonization Index sits inside an ESG factor that multiplies Board bonuses.

Unilever adds a shareholder "say on climate" vote on its transition plan every three years. Stellantis' disclosed annual-incentive metrics found here emphasise quality and financials; no CO₂ metric was found.

### Cited Findings
- **BMW:**
  - The 2025 remuneration report puts about 37% of variable target pay for the 2025 vesting year on ESG targets, with an actual ESG-linked payout share of about 3%.
  - The LTI includes an EU fleet-CO₂ target with a 92.5 g/km threshold and 25% weighting.
  - Sources: [BMW Remuneration Report 2025](https://www.bmwgroup.com/en/report/2025/downloads/BMW-Group-Remuneration-Report-2025-en.pdf); [MarketScreener presentation](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/bmw-presentation-on-the-remuneration-report-2025-ce7e5ed9da8bf421)
  - The ~37% / ~3% figures come from a search summary; verify them in the PDF.
  - BMW reported an EU fleet figure of 90.0 g/km in 2025 vs a 92.9 g/km limit: [BMW press](https://www.press.bmwgroup.com/global/article/detail/T0455333EN/bmw-group-once-again-reduces-eu-co2-fleet-wide-emissions-in-2025)
  - For 2024, BMW openly reported that fleet targets were exceeded while RoCE and BEV-sales targets were missed: [BMW Remuneration Report 2024](https://bmwgroup.com/en/report/2024/downloads/BMW-Group-Remuneration-Report-2024-en.pdf)
- **Volkswagen:**
  - ESG factor = (Environment subtarget, i.e. the Decarbonization Index, × 50% + Social subtarget × 50%) × Governance factor (0.9–1.1). The minimum, target and maximum values correspond to subtarget achievement of 0.7, 1.0 and 1.3.
  - The Decarbonization Index covers life-cycle CO₂ of passenger cars and LCVs in Europe, China and the USA.
  - Sources: [VW AR 2021 remuneration [dated]](https://annualreport2021.volkswagenag.com/corporate-governance/remuneration-report/board-of-management-remuneration.html); [VW AR 2023 sustainability](https://annualreport2023.volkswagenag.com/group-management-report/sustainable-value-enhancement/sustainability.html)
  - The 2025 remuneration page exists, but 2025 achievement values were not retrieved: [VW AR 2025 remuneration](https://annualreport2025.volkswagen-group.com/corporate-governance/remuneration-report/board-of-management-remuneration.html)
  - Secondary source: DI 46.3 t CO₂e/vehicle in 2025 vs a 48.5 t target (prior round; unverified): [GreentechLead](https://greentechlead.com/sustainability/volkswagen-sustainability-strategy-targets-net-zero-by-2050-with-90-emissions-reduction-100-renewable-electricity-and-40-circular-materials-usage-53735)
  - The ESG link to pay was announced in Dec 2020: [Fortune [dated]](https://fortune.com/2020/12/19/volkswagen-vw-link-executive-bonus-esg)
- **Schneider:** ESG achievements feed short- and long-term incentives, including SSERI based on CDP, DJSI, EcoVadis and Vigeo ratings, and results are published with financial results (prior round): [se.com 2023 PR](https://www.se.com/ww/en/about-us/newsroom/news/press-releases/Schneider-Electric-outperforms-2023-sustainability-targets-and-maintains-its-leadership-in-ESG-ratings-65cc89b3ade55370f80f3013)
- **Unilever:**
  - Holds an advisory shareholder vote on its CTAP (first FTSE 100 to do so, 2021), with an updated plan put to a vote every three years. More than 99% support was reported.
  - As You Sow criticised the three-year cadence as too infrequent.
  - Sources: [BusinessGreen](https://businessgreen.com/news/4030848/landmark-vote-cent-unilever-shareholders-climate-transition-plan); [Food in Canada](https://foodincanada.com/sustainability/unilever-shareholders-get-vote-on-climate-action-but-as-you-sow-says-not-often-enough-147073); [Trellis [dated]](https://trellis.net/article/unilever-sets-out-net-zero-plans-shareholder-vote/)
- **Natura:** IP&L is used as a management and investor-reporting tool, putting impact in the same currency as P&L: [IFAC](https://www.ifac.org/knowledge-gateway/discussion/case-study-integrated-profit-and-loss-accounting-natura-co)
- **Stellantis:**
  - The FY2025 20-F says annual incentive metrics "include ESG performance measures". Quality (product quality, service quality and customer satisfaction, warranty cost) has seen its weighting increase each year since 2021.
  - Previously set targets were maintained despite restructuring.
  - Sources: [Stellantis 20-F FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1605484/000160548426000021/stellantis-20251231.htm); [Remuneration Report 2025](https://www.stellantis.com/content/dam/stellantis-corporate/investors/stock-and-shareholder-info/shareholder-meetings/agm-2026/Stellantis-Remuneration-Report-2025.pdf)
  - The 2024 remuneration report received only 66.92% approval (same sources, via search summary).
  - No CO₂ metric in the bonus was found (unverified either way).
  - FaSTLAne 2030 (21 May 2026) headline releases contain no decarbonisation targets (prior round): [Stellantis press](https://www.stellantis.com/en/news/press-releases/2026/may/stellantis-unveils-60-billion-euro-strategic-plan-to-accelerate-growth-and-profit)

### Inferences
- **Pay is the strongest accountability signal,** but the BMW detail of "~37% ESG-linked target, ~3% actual payout" shows how pay links can look bigger than they are. A transparent narrative should show actual payout, not just weighting.
- **Stellantis:** with "customer trust/quality" already the growing pay metric, Stellantis could build an accountability story that combines quality and CO₂ in one disclosed scorecard. That would make territory 4 reinforce territory 2 (Customer Trust).

### Gaps
- Stellantis' bonus and PSU ESG metric names and weights were not read (the PDF was not accessible).
- Mercedes and Renault pay links to CO₂ were not found in this round.
- Board sustainability committee structures were not compared.

## Q5. The 3–5 strongest cases, and why

### Takeaway
1. **Schneider Electric.** Rhythm plus one number plus pay.
